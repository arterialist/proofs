# September 2026 compact-window result and the actual growing carrier

Primary source: Xuefeng Zhu, [Weil positivity in compact windows, v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24827v2), arXiv v2 uploaded 2 September 2026; the PDF is dated 3 September 2026. RH Agent3 supplied the scope audit; RH Proof independently checked the primary theorem statements and the actual packet geometry. This audit checks theorem scope; it does not independently validate its interval computations or import its conclusions into Lean.

Theorem 1.2 reports Q(f)>=8.9e-18||f||^2 for real even f supported in [-0.8,0.8]; Corollary 6.3 extends positivity to complex tests in that window. Theorem 1.1 gives a finite reduction requiring a positive leading matrix eigenvalue and explicit tail/coupling control. It is not a proof of those conditions at every window size. The paragraph after Theorem 1.2 retracts the earlier claimed support-2.38 certificate, retaining only an exploratory computation there. Theorem 1.3's larger-window asymptotic is conditional on RH. Theorem 1.4 bounds the frequency threshold for pointwise prime-comb envelopes; it does not exclude other signed mechanisms or prove RH impossible by finite reductions.

## Exact failure of the direct carrier consumer

The [actual q-flat packet](q-flat-all-place-signed-compensation.md) is f_J=sum_(j=0)^(J+1)c_j eta(.-jL), L=log2, c_0=c_(J+1)=1, eta(0)>0, and eta has support width d<=1/5<L, as constructed in [the gamma-binary mechanism test](gamma-binary-partial-form-counterexample.md). At v=0 only j=0 survives, so f_J(0)=eta(0)>0. At v=(J+1)L only the terminal packet survives, so f_J((J+1)L)=eta(0)>0. Any interval containing its support consequently has length at least (J+1)log2. For J>=3 this is at least 4log2>2>1.6. Translation preserves the separation of these two nonzero values. More generally, the same argument works for any nonzero seed supported in an interval of width less than L: choose a point v_0 where eta(v_0) is nonzero. Only the first packet survives at v_0 and only the terminal packet survives at v_0+(J+1)L. Thus this support obstruction does not depend on positivity or the choice of the seed center. The inequality log2>1/2 follows by integrating 1/x on [1,2].

Therefore no translation of this actual carrier fits the certified [-0.8,0.8] window. The reported certificate cannot directly supply its full signed positivity. Splitting into shorter packets retains mixed full-form terms, which the small-window theorem alone does not bound. The finite reduction can in principle be set up at the larger width, but its leading-matrix lower bound and tail margins would require a new proof or validated calculation; neither is supplied here.

This is a consumer obstruction, not a refutation of the preprint. No R_J(E)<=K_J estimate or RH proof follows. The new window result does not change the unresolved signed arithmetic step for the same growing gamma-binary family.

The support-separation argument is a reviewed written proof, not Lean. No certificate matrix was recomputed, no claimed numerical margin was independently validated, and no paper theorem was added as a Lean axiom. A larger-window arithmetic estimate or a proof controlling all mixed packet terms remains necessary for this consumer.
