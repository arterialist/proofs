# Research notes

Each folder has a short introduction and an alphabetical list of notes. The notes include proved results, conditional RH criteria, and counterexamples; check the proof status in each one.

## Counting and prime histories

- [Successor and division](successor-and-division/) connects the $+1$ order with divisibility.
- [Prime histories](prime-history/) follows a prime's later multiples and mixed-prime descendants.
- [Factorial and renewal](factorial-and-renewal/) studies division histories, factorial weights, and renewal equations.
- [Prime distribution](prime-distribution/) contains Möbius sums, covariance, sieve estimates, and prime-counting error.

## Analytic routes to RH

- [Zeta and zeros](zeta-and-zeros/) contains explicit formulas and conditional RH criteria.
- [Weil and spectral methods](weil-and-spectral/) studies complete prime-power forms and their spectra.
- [Theta and heat](theta-and-heat/) studies theta and xi kernels and zero-location tests.
- [Goldbach and additive pairs](goldbach/) studies additive prime pairs and centered heat identities.
- [Analytic methods](analytic-methods/) collects Fourier, Mellin, and matrix estimates used across these routes.

## Other models

- [Geometry](geometry/) contains cyclotomic, Farey, and lattice constructions.
- [Dynamics and feedback](dynamics-and-feedback/) tests source, response, and conservation models.

The [arithmetic subject index](arithmetic-subject-index.md) connects results across folders. The [long catalog](catalog.md) gives detailed summaries and cross-references.
