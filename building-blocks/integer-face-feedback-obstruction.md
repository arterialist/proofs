# Positive face propagation does not repair the integer renewal determinant

This note gives a finite obstruction for one specified Gram-resolvent coupling on the successor/division graph. It does not exclude other couplings. The proof uses ordinary incidence, block elimination and formal power series; it is not a Lean formalization and carries no publication-priority claim.

Use vertices $1,\ldots,N$, successor edges of weight $x$, and every division $dn\to n$ of weight $-q_d$, where $q_d=\prod_pz_p^{\nu_p(d)}$. Let $B$ be the weighted incidence matrix

```math
Be_e=e_{\mathrm{tail}(e)}-w(e)e_{\mathrm{head}(e)}.
```

For a path $P=e_1\cdots e_k$, let $c(P)=\sum_j(\prod_{i<j}w(e_i))e_{e_j}$. Telescoping gives $Bc(P)=e_{\rm start}-w(P)e_{\rm end}$.

Include all composition faces comparing $abn\to bn\to n$ with $abn\to n$, and all affine faces comparing $mn\to n\to n+1$ with $mn\to\cdots\to m(n+1)\to n+1$, within the cutoff. The face matrix $C$ subtracts these path chains. Then

```math
\Omega:=BC,\qquad
\Omega_{T_{a,b,n}}=-2q_{ab}e_n,\qquad
\Omega_{F_{m,n}}=q_m(x-x^m)e_{n+1}.
```

These are exact endpoint defects, not a boundary-square-zero chain complex. If $J$ duplicates vertex values onto outgoing edges and $V=\operatorname{diag}(1-d_{\rm out})$, the renewal matrix is $A=V+BJ=I-T^T$, where $T$ is row-source weighted adjacency.

## Independent faces and interacting faces

For a composition face,

```math
C_T=e_{D_{a,bn}}-q_a e_{D_{b,n}}-e_{D_{ab,n}},
\qquad C_T^TJ=-q_ae_{bn}^T.
```

Hence $\Omega_TC_T^TJ=2q_{a^2b}e_ne_{bn}^T$. Every such term belongs to the ideal generated by the squares $z_p^2$. The independent-face update $A+\Omega C^TJ$ therefore preserves every squarefree coefficient of its normalized formal log determinant at $x=1$, when affine curvature vanishes. It cannot remove a pre-existing squarefree mixed-prime defect at that clock.

Now allow faces to interact through the fixed mass-one resolvent:

```math
H=(I+C^*C)^{-1},\quad
A_{\rm eff}=A+\Omega HC^*J.
```

For finite complex parameters $0<H\le I$. Also

```math
CHC^*=I-(I+CC^*)^{-1},
\qquad
A_{\rm eff}=V+B\bigl(2I-(I+CC^*)^{-1}\bigr)J.
```

The middle operator is positive, but $B$ and $J$ are not adjoints. Positivity of the vertex correction does not follow. For formal holomorphic coefficients use $C^T$ in place of $C^*$; this agrees at real parameters but has no general positivity assertion at complex ones.

At $x=1$, write subscripts for total prime-marker degree. Then $\Omega_0=\Omega_1=0$, $A_0=I-S^T$, and

```math
[\log\det A_{\rm eff}-\log\det A]_2
=\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(A_0^{-1}\Omega_2(I+C_0^TC_0)^{-1}C_0^TJ\bigr).
```

This follows by expanding $\log\det(I+A^{-1}(A_{\rm eff}-A))$: quadratic powers of the update start at degree four, and variations of the other factors start at degree three. Shared-face propagation can thus change squarefree degree-two coefficients even though independent-face feedback cannot.

All linear coefficients remain unchanged:

```math
[z_p]\log\det A_{\rm eff}=\lfloor N/p\rfloor\quad(p\le N).
```

Indeed the update begins at degree two. The original linear coefficient counts the single ratio-$p$ rods $[m,pm]$ in the determinant expansion. It already differs from the Euler-log coefficient 1 whenever $N\ge2p$.

## An explicit four-state obstruction

Set $N=4$, $x=1$, $u=z_2$, $v=z_3$. There are two faces, $T_{2,2,1}$ and $F_{2,1}$. Their transpose Gram matrix is

```math
G=I+C^TC=
\begin{pmatrix}3+u^2&-1-u\\-1-u&5+u^2\end{pmatrix},
\quad g(u)=\det G=u^4+7u^2-2u+14.
```

Substituting the two path columns into $\Omega G^{-1}C^TJ$ gives the rank-one correction $e_1r^T$, where

```math
r^T=\frac{2u^2}{g(u)}
\bigl(u(1+u),\ u(5+u^2),\ 1+u,\ 1+u\bigr).
```

For real arithmetic weights $u=2^{-s}>0$, its symmetric part has the principal minor on coordinates 1 and 3

```math
-\frac{u^4(1+u)^2}{g(u)^2}<0.
```

Thus the vertex correction is indefinite despite the positive face resolvent.

The unmodified determinant is $D_4=1+2u+v+u^2$. The same two-column calculation, or the rank-one determinant formula, gives

```math
D_{\rm eff}=D_4+
\frac{4u^2(u^3+u^2+4u+1)}{u^4+7u^2-2u+14}.
```

Its quadratic correction is $2u^2/7$, so

```math
[u^2]\log D_{\rm eff}=-5/7,\qquad
[uv]\log D_{\rm eff}=-2.
```

The corresponding Euler-log coefficients are $1/2$ and 0. The natural root-deleted cofactor remains $Q_4=1+u$, because the correction is supported on row 1. Root normalization fixes the linear prime coefficients but still gives

```math
[u^2]\log(D_{\rm eff}/Q_4)=-3/14,\qquad
[uv]\log(D_{\rm eff}/Q_4)=-2.
```

The selected coupling therefore fails even after the root normalization supplied by the graph.

## Attribution and scope

Weighted connection incidence and its distinction between inverse transport and metric adjoint are established; see [Kenyon, *Spanning forests and the vector bundle Laplacian*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.4028). The Gram and block identities here are elementary linear algebra. The construction-specific results are the squarefree ideal obstruction and the explicit failure of this mass-one face coupling. Neither a positive auxiliary block nor an indefinite correction establishes any statement about actual zeta zeros.
