# Native affine-Toeplitz realization of the Abel tail

This note replaces the imported additive operator in the Bost--Connes
calculation by the concrete affine semigroup Toeplitz algebra itself. The
successor, dilation, radial Abel diagonal, tail resolvent, finite horizon,
and boundary projection all belong to one represented algebra. The Toeplitz
boundary does give an exact negative future-cone flux. That flux is exactly
the rank-one horizon-centering term already present in \(C_q\); on the
complete Möbius admission vector it does not imply multi-prime contraction.

Only the complement

\[
t>T_X(\theta):=
\bigl[\max(|\theta|,X^{-1})\bigr]^{1/2-\eta_\varepsilon}
\tag{1}
\]

is considered below. The enlarged low-clock region is already paid and is
not counted again.

## Affine semigroup and exact division domain

On \(\mathscr H=\ell^2(\mathbb N)\), with basis \(e_1,e_2,\ldots\), put

\[
Se_n=e_{n+1},\qquad V_me_n=e_{mn}.
\tag{2}
\]

Then

\[
V_mS=S^mV_m,\qquad V_mV_n=V_{mn},\qquad V_m^*V_m=I.
\tag{3}
\]

Let \(E_m=V_mV_m^*\). The adjoint \(V_m^*\) is strict division:

\[
V_m^*e_k=
\begin{cases}
e_{k/m},&m\mid k,\\
0,&m\nmid k,
\end{cases}
\qquad
E_me_k=1_{m\mid k}e_k.
\tag{4}
\]

In particular no off-domain division state is added. The interaction with
successor powers is

\[
\boxed{
V_m^*S^kV_m=1_{m\mid k}S^{k/m}.
}
\tag{5}
\]

This retains every residue class and every power \(V_{p^a}=V_p^a\).

Write

\[
P_0=I-SS^*=|e_1\rangle\langle e_1|,
\qquad
P_N=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}S^kP_0S^{*k}.
\tag{6}
\]

Both are native Toeplitz projections. The finite-horizon trace state is

\[
\omega_N(A)=\frac1N\operatorname{Tr}(P_NAP_N).
\tag{7}
\]

## Native Abel tail observable

Fix \(0<q<1\) and write \(r=\sqrt q\). The two operators

\[
D_q=\sum_{k\ge0}q^{(k+1)/2}S^kP_0S^{*k},
\qquad
R_q=(I-rS^*)^{-1}=\sum_{k\ge0}r^k(S^*)^k
\tag{8}
\]

converge in operator norm and belong to the affine Toeplitz algebra. Thus
the Abel radial weight and the future-tail resolvent are native here.

For a coefficient vector \(c=(c_1,\ldots,c_N)\), define the rank-one
source operator

\[
B_{q,N}(c)=\sum_{n\le N}c_nq^{n/2}S^{n-1}P_0
=|D_qc\rangle\langle e_1|,
\tag{9}
\]

and

\[
Y_{q,N}(c)=R_qB_{q,N}(c),
\qquad
\mathcal O_{q,N}(c)=\sqrt{N(1-q)}\,S Y_{q,N}(c).
\tag{10}
\]

All operators in (9)--(10) are in the algebra generated by \(S,V_m\).
The final successor makes the finite trace mean vanish, while preserving
the norm because \(S^*S=I\):

\[
\omega_N(\mathcal O_{q,N}(c))=0.
\tag{11}
\]

Moreover

\[
Y_{q,N}(c)e_1=T_qc,
\qquad
(T_qc)_j=\sum_{n\ge j}c_nq^{n-j/2}.
\tag{12}
\]

Consequently the finite-horizon covariance is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\omega_N\bigl(\mathcal O_{q,N}(c)^*\mathcal O_{q,N}(d)\bigr)
=(1-q)\langle T_qc,T_qd\rangle
=\sum_{n,m\le N}\overline{c_n}d_m C_q(n,m),
}
\tag{13}
\]

where

\[
C_q(n,m)=q^{\max(n,m)}-q^{n+m}.
\tag{14}
\]

Thus the centered Abel tail is a native affine-Toeplitz covariance. Unlike
the BC Gibbs construction, no compensating power \(j^{\beta/2}\), zeta
partition factor, or nonnative additive shift has been inserted.

## Exact dilation and moving endpoints

The radial diagonal and dilation obey

\[
D_qV_m=V_mD_{q^m}.
\tag{15}
\]

The resolvent identity retains all successor residues:

\[
\boxed{
R_qV_m=
\left(\sum_{b=0}^{m-1}q^{b/2}(S^*)^bV_m\right)R_{q^m}.
}
\tag{16}
\]

Indeed split each resolvent power \(k=am+b\) and use
\((S^*)^{am}V_m=V_m(S^*)^a\), including the boundary case. In coordinates,

\[
(T_qV_mc)_j
=\sum_{n\ge\lceil j/m\rceil}c_nq^{mn-j/2}.
\tag{17}
\]

The ceiling in (17) is the literal moving admission endpoint. Formula (16)
does not replace it by an independent dilation fiber.

## Möbius incidence and all powers

For a finite prime set \(\mathcal P\), put

\[
\mathfrak M_{\mathcal P}=\prod_{p\in\mathcal P}(I-V_p).
\tag{18}
\]

Then

\[
P_N\mathfrak M_{\mathcal P}P_0
=\sum_{\substack{n\le N\;\text{squarefree}\\
p\mid n\Rightarrow p\in\mathcal P}}
\mu(n)|e_n\rangle\langle e_1|.
\tag{19}
\]

The zero coefficients at proper powers are the actual Möbius coefficients.
The states and operators \(V_{p^a}\) remain in the algebra and in the
finite trace state; they have not been deleted. Taking \(\mathcal P\) to
contain all primes at most \(N\) gives the exact truncated Möbius source in
(9), and then (13) is the truncated \(V_\mu(q)\).

Before admitting \(p\), let \(c\) be the current source and

\[
Q_p=I-E_p,
\qquad
s^{(p)}=-V_pQ_pc.
\tag{20}
\]

Equations (13) and (17) give

\[
\operatorname{Re}\omega_N
\bigl(\mathcal O(c)^*\mathcal O(s^{(p)})\bigr)
=-\mathcal A_p(q;c),
\tag{21}
\]

with the exact endpoint \(\lceil j/p\rceil\), and

\[
\operatorname{Var}(c+s^{(p)})-\operatorname{Var}(c)
=V_{q^p}[Q_pc]-2\mathcal A_p(q;c).
\tag{22}
\]

Thus the native construction preserves the needed negative cross term, but
does not sign it.

## What the Toeplitz boundary flux actually supplies

For any \(y\in\mathscr H\), the Toeplitz defect gives the genuine negative
future-cone identity

\[
\boxed{
\|S^*y\|^2-\|y\|^2=-\|P_0y\|^2.
}
\tag{23}
\]

Apply this to \(y=T_qc\). If

\[
G_q(n,m)=q^{\max(n,m)},
\tag{24}
\]

then direct polarization of (23) gives

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{n,m}\overline{c_n}d_mG_q(n,m)
=(1-q)\langle T_qc,T_qd\rangle
+q\langle P_0T_qc,P_0T_qd\rangle.
}
\tag{25}
\]

Since

\[
q\langle P_0T_qc,P_0T_qd\rangle
=\sum_{n,m}\overline{c_n}d_mq^{n+m},
\tag{26}
\]

equations (25)--(26) say exactly

\[
C_q=G_q-q^{n+m}.
\tag{27}
\]

Therefore the Toeplitz boundary supplies a real negative flux, but that flux
is precisely the horizon-centering rank-one term already contained in
\(C_q\). It is not an additional negative admission term.

For the special Möbius vector, the boundary amplitude is

\[
\sqrt q\,P_0T_q\mu
=\left(\sum_{n\ge1}\mu(n)q^n\right)e_1.
\tag{28}
\]

This additive Möbius generating function has no Euler factorization. Under
dilation,

\[
\sum_n(V_mc)_nq^n=\sum_nc_nq^{mn},
\tag{29}
\]

so even the boundary row retains the same \(q\mapsto q^m\) affine
correlations. Its square does not decompose into independent prime fluxes.

## Multi-prime interference and the fixed-set kernel

Let \(F\) be the depleted source and let
\(U=\sum_{p\in\mathcal P}U_p\) be the complete coherently admitted vector.
From (13), the full interference is

\[
2\{\|U\|_{C_q}^2-2\operatorname{Re}\langle F,U\rangle_{C_q}\}
=2\|U-F\|_{C_q}^2-2\|F\|_{C_q}^2.
\tag{30}
\]

The boundary part of (30) is the same expression in the one-dimensional
seminorm \(|\sum_nc_nq^n|\). It can have either sign, and the positive Green
part in (25) has no comparison with it. Expanding \(U\) also restores every

\[
4\operatorname{Re}\langle U_p,U_r\rangle_{C_q},\qquad p<r,
\tag{31}
\]

including both moving endpoints. Hence the one-prime negative theorem does
not iterate through the Toeplitz defect.

For the normalized actual fixed cells \(g_{X,d}\), equation (13) gives

\[
\operatorname{Cov}_{\omega_N}
(\mathcal O_{q_X,N}(g_{X,d}),\mathcal O_{q_X,N}(g_{X,e}))
=K_X(d,e)\longrightarrow
K(d,e),
\tag{32}
\]

where

\[
K(d,e)=
\frac{2(|d-e|+1)e^{-2\max(d,e)}-e^{-2(d+e)}}{8de}.
\tag{33}
\]

Thus the native affine-Toeplitz model yields the same fixed-set kernel and,
after Möbius dilation, the same affine matrix

\[
\mathcal I_{a,b}(q)=
\sum_{d,e\ge1}\mu(d)\mu(e)
\{q^{\max(ad,be)}-q^{ad+be}\}.
\tag{34}
\]

Native Toeplitz positivity is exactly positivity of this Gram matrix. A
uniform contraction for (30) is exactly the unresolved source-specific
one-sided inequality in \(\mathcal I_{a,b}\).

## Restriction to the paid complement

Let the original physical source, angular kernel, and weight be absorbed in
the coefficient field \(c_{X,\theta,t}\), with \(q=e^{-t}\). Integrating
(13) only over (1) gives the positive functional

\[
\Phi_{\mathrm{far},X}(A)
=\int\!\int_{t>T_X(\theta)}
w(t)\,\omega_N(A_{\theta,t})\,dt\,d\theta.
\tag{35}
\]

Pointwise application of (13), (21), and (23) is justified first at finite
horizon and then by Abel convergence. Therefore the far-clock covariance is
exactly the original centered Poisson/Green quadratic form restricted to
(1), with its original weight, strict division domains, all powers, and
moving endpoints. The low-clock region contributes nothing to (35).

The restriction does not change the conclusion: the boundary flux in every
remaining fiber is the rank-one centering term (26). After integration it
still leaves the complete affine Möbius matrix (34). Consequently the
Toeplitz boundary projection supplies no additional negative future-cone
work capable of proving the multi-prime far-clock bound.
