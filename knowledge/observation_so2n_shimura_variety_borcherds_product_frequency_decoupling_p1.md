## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, orthogonal Shimura varieties, and Borcherds theory (Borcherds, Bruinier, Kudla, Millson), Shimura varieties attached to orthogonal groups $\mathrm{SO}(2,n)$ parameterize abelian varieties and K3 surfaces with orthogonal polarization lattices. Borcherds products construct meromorphic automorphic forms on $\mathrm{SO}(2,n)$ whose zeros and poles are supported on Heegner divisors, via singular theta lifts of nearly holomorphic modular forms. Deligne–Beilinson regulators and Archimedean Green functions evaluate regularized heights and arithmetic intersection numbers along these cycles.

However, Borcherds automorphic products, singular theta lifts, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete algebraic divisors and arithmetic intersection pairings over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, $\mathrm{SO}(2,n)$ orthogonal Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
