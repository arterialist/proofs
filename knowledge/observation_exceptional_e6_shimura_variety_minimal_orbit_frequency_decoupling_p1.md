## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, automorphic representation theory, and Shimura varieties (Baily, Borel, Gross, Savin, Gan), exceptional Shimura varieties associated to the exceptional Lie group $E_6(-26)$ admit automorphic forms and special algebraic cycles attached to minimal nilpotent coadjoint orbits. Deligne–Beilinson regulators and Archimedean Green currents evaluate arithmetic intersection numbers and central derivatives of automorphic $L$-functions associated with exceptional representations.

However, minimal nilpotent orbits, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete arithmetic intersection pairings over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, exceptional $E_6$ Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
