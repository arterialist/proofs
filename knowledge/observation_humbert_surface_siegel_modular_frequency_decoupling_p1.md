## Summary
In arithmetic algebraic geometry and Siegel modular varieties (Humbert, van der Geer, Kudla, Borcherds, Bruinier), Humbert surfaces $H_D \subset \mathcal{A}_2$ on the moduli space of principally polarized abelian surfaces $\mathcal{A}_2 = \mathbb{H}_2 / \mathrm{Sp}_4(\mathbb{Z})$ parametrize abelian surfaces with real multiplication by an order in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{D})$. The generating series of their cycle classes $\sum [H_D] q^D$ is a Siegel modular form of genus 2, and Borcherds products on orthogonal groups $O(2, 3) \cong \mathrm{PSp}_4(\mathbb{R})$ yield meromorphic modular forms whose divisors are linear combinations of Humbert surfaces.

However, Humbert surfaces and their Kudla green functions evaluate algebraic cycle classes on abelian moduli and Fourier–Jacobi expansions with discrete quadratic indices $D \in \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$. They operate within the algebraic motivic category of Shimura varieties, which decouples completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, Humbert surface pairings provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
