## Summary

However, modular characters arising from loop group coadjoint orbits evaluate automorphic partition functions of 2D conformal field theories. While modular under mapping class groups, modularity alone is fundamentally insufficient to confine the zeros of associated Dirichlet series to the critical line (as evidenced by Davenport–Heilbronn counterexamples to modular zero confinement). Coadjoint orbits parameterize algebraic representations whose spectral invariants decouple completely from microscopic complex zero oscillations of $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
