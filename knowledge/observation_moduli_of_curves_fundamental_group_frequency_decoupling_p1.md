## Summary
In algebraic topology, geometric group theory, and moduli theory (Johnson, Morita, Hain, Looijenga), the mapping class group $\operatorname{Mod}_{g,n}$ and the moduli space of curves $\mathcal{M}_{g,n}$ admit Johnson homomorphisms, Morita traces, unipotent fundamental groups, and tautological rings generated by Miller–Morita–Mumford classes $\kappa_i$. Boundary divisor degenerations evaluate stable nodal curves and Torelli subgroup actions.

However, Johnson homomorphisms, Morita traces, and tautological classes evaluate discrete topological and algebraic intersection invariants over moduli spaces of Riemann surfaces. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, moduli of curves fundamental groups provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
