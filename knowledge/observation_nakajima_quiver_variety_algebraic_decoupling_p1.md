## Summary
In geometric representation theory (Nakajima, Lusztig), Nakajima quiver varieties $\mathfrak{M}_\theta(v, w) = \mu^{-1}(0) ///_\theta G_v$ are constructed as hyperkähler quotients of cotangent bundles of quiver representations. The middle cohomology $\bigoplus_v H_{\mathrm{mid}}(\mathfrak{M}_\theta(v, w), \mathbb{C})$ geometrically realizes integrable highest weight representations of Kac–Moody algebras and quantum loop algebras $U_q(L\mathfrak{g})$.

However, Nakajima quiver varieties parameterize stable finite-dimensional linear representations over algebraic fields. Their cohomology and convolution algebras evaluate algebraic tensor invariants and quantum group actions that are invariant under complex base change and decouple completely from continuous transcendental vertical oscillations $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of the Riemann zeta function $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. The algebraic representation theory of quiver varieties possesses zero transverse coercivity across the critical strip $1/2 < \operatorname{Re}(s) < 1$, providing no mechanism to constrain complex zeros onto the critical line.
