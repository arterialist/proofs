## Summary
In complex algebraic geometry and non-abelian Hodge theory (Simpson, Hitchin, Donaldson, Corlette), the non-abelian Hodge correspondence establishes a real analytic diffeomorphism between the moduli space of flat connections $M_{\mathrm{flat}}(X, G)$ and the moduli space of semistable parabolic Higgs bundles $M_{\mathrm{Higgs}}(X, G)$ over a smooth projective variety or compact Riemann surface $X$. The hyperkähler geometry of Higgs moduli spaces yields twistor lines and twistor $\mathcal{D}$-modules.

However, parabolic Higgs bundles, harmonic metrics, and spectral curves evaluate geometric invariants of bundles over fixed algebraic varieties $X$. Their hyperkähler rotation and twistor structures parameterize moduli spaces of holomorphic objects, which decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of the Riemann zeta function $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, non-abelian Hodge twistor structures and harmonic metrics provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
