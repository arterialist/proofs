## Summary
In Sasaki–Einstein geometry and Calabi–Yau cone singularities (Martelli, Sparks, Yau, Gauntlett, Collins, Székelyhidi), isolated toric Calabi–Yau 3-cones $C(Y)$ admit Sasaki–Einstein metrics on their cross-sections $Y$ via volume minimization of the Einstein–Hilbert functional over the Reeb cone. K-polystability of isolated quotient singularities and non-Archimedean Ding stability characterize the existence of Ricci-flat conical Kähler metrics.

However, K-stability of singular toric Calabi–Yau cones and Sasaki–Einstein volume cross-sections evaluate differential geometric Reeb volume ratios and convex moment polytope geometries over $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, singular toric CY cone volume minimization provides zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
