## Summary
In arithmetic algebraic geometry and modular surfaces (Hirzebruch, Zagier, Kudla, Millson, Bruinier, Yang), Hirzebruch–Zagier cycles $T_N \subset X$ on Hilbert modular surfaces $X = (\mathbb{H} \times \mathbb{H})/\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathcal{O}_K)$ are algebraic curves parametrizing abelian surfaces with real multiplication by $\mathcal{O}_K$. By the celebrated Hirzebruch–Zagier theorem, the generating series of their cohomology classes $\sum [T_N] q^N$ is an elliptic modular form of weight 2 with values in $H^2(X, \mathbb{Q})$, and their arithmetic intersection pairings evaluate Fourier coefficients of modular forms of half-integral weight via the Kudla–Millson theta correspondence.

However, Hirzebruch–Zagier cycles and their arithmetic intersection numbers evaluate discrete Fourier coefficients $c(N)$ of holomorphic modular forms and topological intersection numbers of algebraic divisors. They operate within the discrete harmonic analysis of lattices and automorphic forms of discrete index $N \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$, which decouples completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, Hirzebruch–Zagier cycle pairings provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
