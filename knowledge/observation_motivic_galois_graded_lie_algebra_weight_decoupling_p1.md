## Summary
In motivic Galois theory and polylogarithmic motivic complexes (Goncharov, Deligne, Beilinson), the fundamental Lie algebra of the category of mixed Tate motives $\mathcal{MT}(\mathcal{O}_K)$ is graded by negative weights $\mathfrak{n} = \bigoplus_{n \ge 1} \mathfrak{n}_{-n}$. Goncharov's motivic Lie coalgebra encodes the coproduct of motivic iterated integrals, and regulator maps to Deligne–Beilinson cohomology evaluate higher polylogarithms and Borel regulators at algebraic points.

However, motivic Galois Lie algebras and polylogarithmic motivic complexes evaluate algebraic Lie algebras graded by discrete integer weights $n \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$. They decouple completely from the continuous transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of arithmetic Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Motivic Galois representations and polylogarithmic complexes provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
