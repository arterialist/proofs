## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, Lorentzian Shimura varieties, and hyperbolic monodromy theory (Borcherds, Kudla, Millson), Shimura varieties attached to spin groups of signature $(1,n)$ parameterize hyperbolic reflection lattices and Lorentzian abelian structures. Monodromy systems on $\mathrm{GSpin}(1,n)$ evaluate hyperbolic period integrals, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions along Lorentzian geodesic cycles.

However, $\mathrm{GSpin}(1,n)$ Lorentzian Shimura varieties, hyperbolic monodromy systems, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete algebraic reflection groups and arithmetic intersection pairings over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, $\mathrm{GSpin}(1,n)$ Lorentzian Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
