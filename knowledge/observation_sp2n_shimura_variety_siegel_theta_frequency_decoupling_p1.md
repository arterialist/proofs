## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, symplectic Shimura varieties, and theta function theory (Mumford, Faltings, Chai, Kudla), Siegel modular varieties parameterize principally polarized abelian varieties of dimension $g=n$ endowed with symplectic level structures. Siegel theta characteristics and modular forms on $\mathrm{Sp}(2n)$ evaluate singular theta values, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions along modular subvarieties and toroidal boundary degenerations.

However, $\mathrm{Sp}(2n)$ Siegel modular forms, singular theta characteristics, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete algebraic polarization lattices, modular divisors, and arithmetic intersection pairings over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, $\mathrm{Sp}(2n)$ symplectic Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
