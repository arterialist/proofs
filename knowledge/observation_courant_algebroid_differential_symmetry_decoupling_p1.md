## Summary
In generalized complex geometry (Hitchin, Gualtieri), a Courant algebroid $E \to TM \oplus T^*M$ equipped with a non-degenerate symmetric pairing and a Dorfman bracket provides a framework unifying complex and symplectic geometry via generalized complex structures $\mathcal{J} \in \operatorname{End}(E)$ with $\mathcal{J}^2 = -1$. Formulating Courant algebroids and Dirac structures over arithmetic schemes (such as $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}$) attempts to construct an arithmetic variational principle constraining non-trivial zeros.

However, Courant algebroids and Dirac structures operate as differential-geometric or smooth algebraic symmetries of tangent and cotangent bundles. The algebraic Dirac subbundles and Dorfman bracket identities classify local differential forms and geometric foliations, but decouple completely from transcendental complex vertical oscillations $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\sum n^{-s}$. The differential symmetries of $E$ possess zero transverse coercivity across the critical strip $1/2 < \operatorname{Re}(s) < 1$, providing no analytical restoring force to exclude off-line zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
