## Summary
In low-dimensional topology and representation theory (Khovanov, Khovanov-Rozansky), link categorification lifts polynomial link invariants (such as the Jones polynomial $V(q)$ or HOMFLY-PT polynomial) to bigraded homology groups of chain complexes of graded modules $\bigoplus_{i, j} Kh^{i, j}(L)$, whose graded Euler characteristic recovers the classical polynomial: $\sum_{i, j} (-1)^i q^j \dim Kh^{i, j}(L) = V(q)$. In arithmetic topology (Mazur, Kapranov, Reznikov), prime numbers $p \subset \operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}$ are viewed as knots embedded in a 3-manifold, and Legendre symbols $(p/q)$ correspond to linking numbers.

However, categorified link homologies are discrete algebraic invariants consisting of bigraded abelian groups $\mathbb{Z}^{\oplus k} \oplus \text{torsion}$ over discrete topological spaces. The differential $\partial: Kh^{i, j} \to Kh^{i+1, j}$ counts combinatorial cube-of-resolution cobordisms. When mapped to arithmetic over $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}$, the homology groups capture discrete mod-$p$ ramification and cup-product linking phenomena. In stark contrast, the Riemann Hypothesis is an analytical statement concerning the non-vanishing of an infinite Dirichlet series $\sum n^{-s}$ along the continuous Archimedean continuum $s = 1/2 + \delta + it$. Categorified knot homologies possess purely discrete gradings $(i, j) \in \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$ that are completely invariant under continuous phase variations $t \in \mathbb{R}$, providing zero analytical apparatus to produce transverse restoring coercivity off the critical line.
