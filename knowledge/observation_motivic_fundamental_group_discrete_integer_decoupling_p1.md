## Summary
In arithmetic algebraic geometry and the theory of motives (Deligne, Goncharov, Brown), the motivic fundamental groupoid $\pi_1^{\mathcal{M}}(\mathbb{P}^1 \setminus \{0, 1, \infty\}; v_0, v_1)$ classifies motivic iterated integrals whose period realizations evaluate multiple zeta values $\zeta(s_1, \dots, s_k) = \sum_{n_1 > \dots > n_k \ge 1} n_1^{-s_1} \cdots n_k^{-s_k}$ at positive integer weights $s_j \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$. The motivic Galois group $\operatorname{Gal}^{\mathcal{M}}(\mathcal{MT}(\mathbb{Z}))$ acts faithfully on these motivic periods, preserving the weight and depth filtrations and Drinfel'd associator relations.

However, motivic fundamental groups and multiple zeta values evaluate periods of mixed Tate motives restricted strictly to discrete integer weight vectors. They decouple completely from the continuous transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of arithmetic Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Motivic iterated integrals provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
