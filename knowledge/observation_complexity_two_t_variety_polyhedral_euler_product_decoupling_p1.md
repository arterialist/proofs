## Summary
In algebraic geometry and K-stability (Altmann, Hausen, Süß, Ilten, Vollmert), varieties endowed with an effective torus action of complexity $k = \dim(X) - \dim(T) = 2$ are described combinatorially by proper polyhedral divisors on divisorial fans over a 2-dimensional quotient curve or surface. K-stability of complexity-2 Fano $T$-varieties reduces to computing multi-slice delta invariants and volume-minimization functionals over multi-dimensional convex polyhedral cones.

However, these polyhedral divisors, multi-slice delta invariants, and cone volumes evaluate convex polyhedral geometry and combinatorial Cox ring valuations on algebraic varieties over $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, complexity-2 $T$-variety polyhedral invariants provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
