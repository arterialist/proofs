## Summary
In birational geometry and K-stability of log pairs (Berman, Boucksom, Blum, Jonsson, Liu), the logarithmic Ding functional $D_{(X, B)}(\phi) = E_{-K_X - B}(\phi) - \log \int_X e^{-(\phi - \phi_B)}$ and tropical K-moduli compactifications evaluate uniform Ding stability and non-Archimedean degenerations of polarized pairs $(X, B)$. The stability threshold $D(v)$ determines the existence of conical Kähler–Einstein metrics.

However, logarithmic Ding invariants and tropical K-moduli parameterize degenerations of geometric divisor pairs and birational valuations. They decouple completely from the arithmetic prime factorization over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, logarithmic Ding invariants provide zero transverse mechanism to eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
