## Summary
In geometric analysis and Kähler geometry (Hein, Tian, Yau, Donaldson), complete Ricci-flat Kähler metrics on non-compact manifolds include asymptotically conical (AC) and asymptotically flat (AF) metrics arising on resolutions of cone singularities and equivariant group compactifications $X$ of reductive groups $G$. The existence of such complete metrics and their non-Archimedean Ding stability relate to asymptotic volume ratios and Monge–Ampère energy functionals.

However, asymptotically flat metric potentials and group compactifications evaluate differential geometric volume ratios, curvature decays, and non-Archimedean pluripotential energies. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, Riemannian group compactification geometry provides zero transverse analytical mechanism to eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
