## Summary
In toric contact geometry and Sasaki–Einstein manifolds (Martelli, Sparks, Yau, Futaki, Ono, Wang), the Martelli–Sparks–Yau (MSY) volume functional $V(\xi)$ evaluates the volume of the base of a toric Sasakian cone as a function of the Reeb vector field $\xi$. Minimizing $V(\xi)$ over the Reeb cone is equivalent to the vanishing of the transverse Futaki invariant and yields Ricci-flat Sasaki–Einstein cone metrics.

However, Martelli–Sparks–Yau volume minimization and toric contact geometry evaluate continuous algebraic volume functions of the Reeb vector field and moment cone geometry. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, toric Sasakian MSY volume minimization provides zero transverse analytical mechanism to eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
