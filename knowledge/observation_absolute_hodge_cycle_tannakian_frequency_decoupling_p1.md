## Summary
In algebraic geometry and Hodge theory (Deligne, Milne, Ogus, Voisin), Deligne's absolute Hodge cycles on smooth projective varieties (notably abelian varieties) generate a Tannakian category of motives for absolute Hodge cycles $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{AH}}(k)$ endowed with fiber functors to de Rham and Betti cohomology. Comparison isomorphisms between Betti and de Rham cohomology evaluate periods of algebraic differential forms over topological cycles.

However, absolute Hodge cycles and their motivic Galois groups evaluate algebraic cycle classes and discrete $\mathbb{Q}$-representations of reductive Tannakian groups over number fields and $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, absolute Hodge Tannakian structures provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
