## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, spin Shimura varieties, and Kuga–Satake theory (Kuga, Satake, Deligne, Madapusi Pera), Shimura varieties attached to general spin groups $\mathrm{GSpin}(3,n)$ parameterize abelian varieties with Clifford multiplication and Hodge structures of K3 type. Monodromy systems on $\mathrm{GSpin}(3,n)$ evaluate Kuga–Satake period pairings, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions along spin modular subvarieties.

However, $\mathrm{GSpin}(3,n)$ spin Shimura varieties, Kuga–Satake abelian varieties, Clifford algebra spin representations, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete algebraic polarization lattices, Hodge structures, and arithmetic intersection pairings over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, $\mathrm{GSpin}(3,n)$ spin Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
