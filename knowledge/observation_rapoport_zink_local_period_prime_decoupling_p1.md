## Summary
In arithmetic geometry and the Langlands program (Rapoport–Zink, Faltings, Scholze–Weinstein), Rapoport–Zink spaces are non-Archimedean moduli spaces parameterizing deformations of $p$-divisible groups with additional structures. Through Grothendieck–Messing theory and Berkovich/rigid uniformization, they define non-Archimedean period morphisms $\pi: \mathcal{M} \to \mathscr{F}^{\mathrm{wa}}$ into weakly admissible period domains.

However, Rapoport–Zink period spaces categorize local Galois representations and $p$-divisible groups strictly localized at a single prime $p$. The period maps evaluate local Frobenius eigenvalues and Hodge–Tate filtrations in local Fontaine period rings. These non-Archimedean period spaces decouple completely across distinct primes $p \ne \ell$ and lack any global Archimedean vertical continuation along $s = 1/2 + it$. Consequently, Rapoport–Zink theory provides zero transverse mechanism to establish zero-free bounds for $\zeta(s)$ in the complex strip.
