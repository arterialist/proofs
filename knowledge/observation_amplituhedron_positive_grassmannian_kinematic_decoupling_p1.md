## Summary
In gauge theory and scattering amplitudes (Arkani-Hamed, Trnka, Postnikov), the amplituhedron $\mathcal{A}_{n, k, m}(Z)$ is a geometric object in the Grassmannian whose canonical differential forms $\Omega_{n, k, 4}$ evaluate scattering amplitudes in planar $\mathcal{N}=4$ super Yang–Mills theory via positive geometries. The volume forms and logarithmic boundaries compute rational functions of kinematic spinor-helicity momentum twistors $Z_i \in \mathbb{CP}^3$.

However, these kinematic forms evaluate tree and loop integrands of massless quantum gauge fields in four dimensions. They decouple completely from the continuous transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of arithmetic Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Amplituhedron boundaries provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain the complex root distribution of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
