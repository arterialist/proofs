## Summary
In higher-dimensional birational geometry and valuative stability (Birkar–Cascini–Hacon–McKernan, Blum–Jonsson, Liu–Xu), the logarithmic delta invariant $\delta(X, B) = \inf_E \frac{A_{(X, B)}(E)}{S_{(X, B)}(E)}$ evaluates the stability threshold of Kawamata log terminal (klt) pairs $(X, B)$, where $A_{(X, B)}(E)$ is the log discrepancy and $S_{(X, B)}(E)$ is the average vanishing order along prime divisors $E$ over $X$. Uniform stability ($\delta(X, B) > 1$) characterizes the existence of conical Kähler–Einstein metrics.

However, the log delta invariant $\delta(X, B)$ computes birational volume thresholds and singularities of boundary divisors $B$ on algebraic varieties. It characterizes the algebraic stability of geometric pairs under birational degenerations. This threshold invariant decouples completely from the multiplicative structure of the prime numbers $\{p\}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, logarithmic delta invariants provide zero transverse mechanism to eliminate off-line complex roots of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
