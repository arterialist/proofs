## Summary
In hyperkähler differential geometry and gauge theory (Kronheimer, Nakajima, Hitchin, Bielawski, Goto), toric hyperkähler manifolds and Nakajima quiver varieties $\mathfrak{M}_0(v, w)$ arise as hyperkähler quotients $\mathbb{H}^d /// G$ via Tri-Hamiltonian moment maps $(\mu_{\mathbb{R}}, \mu_{\mathbb{C}})$. They admit complete Ricci-flat hyperkähler metrics asymptotic to ALE (asymptotically locally Euclidean) or ALF (asymptotically locally flat) spaces, with hyperkähler K-energy functionals governing metric stability.

However, hyperkähler metric quotients and Nakajima quiver varieties evaluate smooth differential geometric Ricci-flat metrics and moment map reductions over $\mathbb{H}$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, hyperkähler metric geodesics and moment map reductions provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
