## Summary
In categorical algebraic geometry and non-commutative geometry (Kontsevich, Tabuada, Blumberg-Gepner-Tabuada), the theory of non-commutative motives associates to every small differential graded category $\mathcal{A}$ its non-commutative motive $U(\mathcal{A})$ in the universal additive category $\operatorname{Hmo}_0$. By Tabuada's universality theorem, localizing invariants such as algebraic $K$-theory $K_*(\mathcal{A})$, cyclic homology $HC_*(\mathcal{A})$, and periodic cyclic homology $HP_*(\mathcal{A})$ factor uniquely through non-commutative motives.

However, non-commutative motives are constructed by formally linearizing dg-categories via Morita equivalences and Verdier localizations into an additive or stable $(\infty, 1)$-category. The pairings computed by cyclic homology and non-commutative Chern characters are algebraic pairings between cyclic homology classes and non-commutative differential forms. Crucially, the non-trivial zeros of $\zeta(s)$ are governed by continuous, transcendental vertical oscillations $t = \operatorname{Im}(s) \in \mathbb{R}$. In the additive category $\operatorname{Hmo}_0$, algebraic tensor products and morphism groups decouple completely from continuous vertical frequency shifts $s \mapsto s + it$. Non-commutative motives possess zero Archimedean metric, continuous spectral flow, or transverse restoring force off the critical line, failing to provide any analytical leverage to exclude off-line complex zeros.
