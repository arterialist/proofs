## Summary

The boundary regulator evaluates Eisenstein symbols directly into Deligne–Beilinson cohomology.

However, Beilinson's Eisenstein symbols and boundary regulator maps evaluate discrete arithmetic invariants associated with modular units and cuspidal boundary strata over number fields $\mathbb{Q}$. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, cuspidal Eisenstein symbols provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
