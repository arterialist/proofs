## Summary
In algebraic geometry and K-stability (Delcroix, Pezzini, Fujita, Blum, Kaveh, Khovanskii), a spherical variety $X$ under a reductive group $G$ has an open $B$-orbit. Newton–Okounkov bodies $\Delta(X, D)$ associated to $G$-stable boundary divisors encode the asymptotic distribution of section spaces $H^0(X, kD)$. K-stability of spherical Fano varieties reduces to a volume-minimization problem for a modified Duistermaat–Heckman measure on the colored moment cone $\mathcal{C}(X)$, where the measure is weighted by the product of positive roots and colored valuations.

However, Newton–Okounkov bodies, colored moment cones, and spherical root valuations evaluate convex polyhedral volumes and combinatorial representation data of reductive groups over $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, spherical Fano Okounkov cone invariants provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
