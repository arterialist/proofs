## Summary
In algebraic geometry and K-stability (Altmann, Hausen, Süß, Ilten), Fano varieties endowed with an effective torus action of complexity $k = \dim(X) - \dim(T) = 35$ are parameterized combinatorially by multi-fan divisorial systems and Cox ring quotients over a 35-dimensional base. K-stability of complexity-35 Fano $T$-varieties reduces to volume minimization and delta invariant computations over high-dimensional multi-polyhedral cones.

However, these 35-dimensional polyhedral fan valuations evaluate convex polyhedral cone volumes and birational stability thresholds of algebraic varieties over $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, complexity-35 $T$-variety polyhedral cone invariants provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
