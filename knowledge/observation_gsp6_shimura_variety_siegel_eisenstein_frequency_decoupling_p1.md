## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, Siegel modular threefolds, and boundary Eisenstein cohomology (Siegel, Satake, Faltings, Chai), Shimura varieties attached to the general symplectic group $\mathrm{GSp}(6)$ parameterize principally polarized abelian threefolds. Siegel–Eisenstein distributions evaluate Eisenstein series residues and boundary cycle classes along lower-dimensional cusp strata (Siegel, Klingen, and Borel cusps), while Deligne–Beilinson regulators evaluate Archimedean Green currents and arithmetic intersection pairings.

However, Siegel–Eisenstein boundary distributions, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete algebraic cohomology classes and boundary residues over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, $\mathrm{GSp}(6)$ Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
