## Summary
In arithmetic intersection theory, modular forms, and algebraic surfaces (Hirzebruch, Zagier, van der Geer, Bruinier), Hilbert modular surfaces $Y(K)$ associated with real quadratic fields $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{D})$ contain algebraic cycles $T_N$ (Hirzebruch–Zagier curves). Deligne–Beilinson regulators, automorphic Borcherds products, and Archimedean Green currents evaluate arithmetic intersections and boundary cusp monodromy.

However, Hirzebruch–Zagier algebraic cycle intersections, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete arithmetic invariants and geometric intersection numbers over Hilbert modular surfaces. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, Hilbert modular surfaces provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
