## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, Picard surfaces, and monodromy theory (Picard, Lefschetz, Deligne, Mostow), Picard modular surfaces attached to unitary groups $U(2,1)$ over imaginary quadratic fields admit families of abelian varieties with Picard–Lefschetz monodromy around boundary divisors and cusps. Deligne–Beilinson regulators and Archimedean Green functions evaluate arithmetic intersection pairings and vanishing cycle periods.

However, Picard–Lefschetz vanishing cycles, monodromy operators, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete topological monodromy and arithmetic intersection numbers over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, $U(2,1)$ Picard surfaces provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
