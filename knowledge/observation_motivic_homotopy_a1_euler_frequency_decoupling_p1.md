## Summary
In motivic homotopy theory (Morel, Voevodsky, Hoyois, Levine, Bachmann), the $\mathbb{A}^1$-homotopy category $\mathcal{H}(k)$ and stable motivic homotopy category $\mathcal{SH}(k)$ model algebraic varieties and smooth quotient stacks $[X/G]$. The categorical Euler characteristic of a smooth projective variety or smooth quotient stack takes values in the Grothendieck–Witt ring of quadratic forms $GW(k)$, computing quadratic refinements of classical topological degrees.

However, motivic homotopy theory and $\mathbb{A}^1$-Euler characteristics evaluate quadratic forms over the ground field $k$ and stable motivic homotopy groups. These algebraic structures decouple completely from the continuous transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of arithmetic Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Motivic homotopy sheaves and $\mathbb{A}^1$-degrees provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
