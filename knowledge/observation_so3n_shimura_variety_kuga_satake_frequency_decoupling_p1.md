## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, orthogonal Shimura varieties, and Kuga–Satake theory (Kuga, Satake, Deligne, Kudla), Shimura varieties attached to orthogonal groups $\mathrm{SO}(3,n)$ parameterize polarized Hodge structures of K3 type. The Kuga–Satake construction embeds these orthogonal Shimura varieties into symplectic Shimura varieties parameterizing abelian varieties via the Clifford algebra $C(V)$ and its spin representations. Deligne–Beilinson regulators and Archimedean Green functions evaluate regularized heights and arithmetic intersection numbers along Kuga–Satake cycles.

However, $\mathrm{SO}(3,n)$ orthogonal Shimura varieties, Kuga–Satake abelian varieties, Clifford algebra spin representations, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete algebraic polarization lattices, Hodge structures, and arithmetic intersection pairings over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, $\mathrm{SO}(3,n)$ orthogonal Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
