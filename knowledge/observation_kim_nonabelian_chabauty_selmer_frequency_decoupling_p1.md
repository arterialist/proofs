## Summary
In arithmetic geometry and the theory of motivic fundamental groups (Minhyong Kim, Coates, Wiles, Faltings), Kim's non-abelian Chabauty method uses unipotent fundamental groups $\pi_1^{\mathrm{un}}(X)$ and unipotent Albanese varieties to construct non-abelian Selmer varieties $\operatorname{Sel}(X)$. Obstruction functions arising from $p$-adic polylogarithms cut out finite sets of rational points on hyperbolic curves.

However, unipotent Selmer variety obstruction loci and $p$-adic polylogarithms evaluate algebraic unipotent Galois representations and $p$-adic periods over number fields. They specialize to discrete local arithmetic conditions that decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, Kim's Selmer variety obstruction loci provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
