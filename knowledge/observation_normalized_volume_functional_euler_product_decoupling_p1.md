## Summary
In local birational geometry and K-stability of singularities (Chi Li, Chenyang Xu, Blum, Liu), the normalized volume functional $\widehat{\operatorname{vol}}(v) = A(v)^n \operatorname{vol}(v)$ evaluates the normalized volume of an arbitrary valuation $v$ centered at a Kawamata log terminal (klt) singularity $x \in X$. The unique minimizing valuation $v_0$ induces a canonical K-semistable degeneration to the metric tangent cone.

However, normalized volume minimizers compute local algebraic multiplicity thresholds and volumes of graded valuation ideals in local rings $\mathcal{O}_{X, x}$. They characterize local algebraic singularity types under birational degenerations. This local birational invariant decouples completely from the prime factorization over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the global infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, normalized volume functionals provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
