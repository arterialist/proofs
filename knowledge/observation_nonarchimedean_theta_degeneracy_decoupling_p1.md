## Summary
In arithmetic geometry and toroidal compactifications (Faltings–Chai, Mumford), the boundary of the moduli space of principally polarized abelian varieties $\mathcal{A}_g$ over $\mathbb{Z}$ is stratified by degenerating semi-abelian schemes. Over complete non-Archimedean fields $K$ (such as $\mathbb{Q}_p$), non-Archimedean theta functions $\vartheta(z, \tau) = \sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}^g} q^{B(n, n)/2} w^n$ converge on non-Archimedean tori $\mathbb{G}_m^g / Y$, where $Y \cong \mathbb{Z}^g$ is a discrete period lattice.

However, non-Archimedean theta functions and degeneration charts operate strictly locally at single non-Archimedean places $p$. The boundary degeneration data evaluate discrete combinatorial tropical skeleta and valuation valuations $\operatorname{val}(q_i)$. These non-Archimedean period lattices decouple across distinct primes and provide zero continuous Archimedean vertical analytic continuation or spectral mechanism along $s = 1/2 + it$ for $t \in \mathbb{R}$. Automorphic modular forms arising from Siegel theta series evaluate algebraic Fourier expansions whose modular invariance is strictly independent of the vertical distribution of non-trivial zeros off the critical line (as evidenced by Davenport–Heilbronn counterexamples).
