## Summary
In algebraic geometry and motivic cohomology (Spencer Bloch, Soulé, Beilinson, Suslin), higher Chow groups $\operatorname{CH}^p(X, q)$ compute motivic cohomology $H^{2p-q}_M(X, \mathbb{Z}(p))$. The regulator maps of Soulé and Beilinson map higher Chow cycles to Deligne–Beilinson cohomology $H_{\mathcal{D}}^{2p-q}(X, \mathbb{R}(p))$.

However, higher Chow regulator classes evaluate discrete algebraic cycle combinations and formal regulator periods over number fields. They specialize to discrete algebraic classes that decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, higher Chow regulator images provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
