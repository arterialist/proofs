## Summary
In arithmetic algebraic geometry and Arakelov theory (Deligne, Beilinson, Gillet, Soulé, Faltings), Deligne–Beilinson cohomology $H^1_{\mathcal{D}}(X, \mathbb{R}(1))$ on Shimura curves parameterizes arithmetic divisor classes endowed with smooth Archimedean Green currents. The arithmetic intersection pairing and Néron–Tate height of CM points evaluate heights of algebraic cycles and special cycles under the Arakelov metric.

However, Archimedean Green functions on compact Riemann surfaces and Néron–Tate heights of CM cycles evaluate geometric intersection numbers and automorphic Green currents at fixed arithmetic points over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, Shimura curve Arakelov heights provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
