## Summary
In algebraic and symplectic geometry (Okounkov, Kaveh–Khovanskii, Lazarsfeld–Mustaţă), the Newton–Okounkov body $\Delta(X, D) \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ associated with a big divisor $D$ on a projective variety $X$ and a full flag of subvarieties evaluates asymptotic valuation vectors and volume formulas $\operatorname{vol}(D) = n! \operatorname{vol}_{\mathbb{R}^n}(\Delta(X, D))$. It induces flat toric degenerations to projective toric varieties.

However, Newton–Okounkov bodies evaluate convex polyhedral geometry and birational volumes of linear systems on algebraic varieties. They decouple completely from the prime factorization over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, Okounkov bodies provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
