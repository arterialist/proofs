## Summary
Toeplitz operators $T_\phi = P_+ M_\phi P_+$ on Hardy spaces $H^2$ with unimodular phase symbols $\phi(t) = \zeta(1/2+it)/\zeta(1/2-it) = e^{2i\theta(t)}$ encounter a divergent Riemann-Siegel phase winding $\theta(t) \sim \frac{t}{2}\log \frac{t}{2\pi}$. By the Hartman-Wintner theorem, symbols with infinite winding have essential spectra that fill the entire closed unit disk $\mathbb{D}$. The operator $T_\phi$ is severely non-Fredholm, destroying index stability and completely submerging local $O(1)$ off-line zero phase perturbations into the dense continuous spectrum.
