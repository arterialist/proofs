## Summary
In arithmetic topology and representation varieties (Goldman, Kapovich–Millson, Sikora), character varieties $\mathcal{X}(M, G) = \operatorname{Hom}(\pi_1(M), G) // G$ of 3-manifolds $M$ or arithmetic schemes $\operatorname{Spec} \mathcal{O}_K$ carry a canonical Goldman symplectic form $\omega_G$ and Poisson brackets $\{ \operatorname{tr}_\alpha, \operatorname{tr}_\beta \}$ on trace coordinates. These geometric structures classify conjugacy classes of representations of the fundamental group.

However, character varieties parameterize algebraic moduli spaces of representations into reductive algebraic groups. The Goldman symplectic bracket reflects intersection pairings in group homology $H_1(M; \mathfrak{g}_{\mathrm{Ad}})$. These finite-dimensional algebraic symplectic structures decouple completely from the continuous transcendental oscillation frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of arithmetic Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Character varieties and trace brackets carry zero transverse analytic coercivity to penalize or eliminate off-critical zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
