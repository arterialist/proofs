## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, automorphic representation theory, and Shimura varieties (Gross, Wallach, Gan, Savin), exceptional Shimura varieties attached to the real form $E_{7(-133)}$ admit automorphic representations generating quaternionic discrete series and associated algebraic cycles. Deligne–Beilinson regulators and Archimedean Green functions evaluate arithmetic intersection pairings and central values of automorphic $L$-functions associated with exceptional quaternionic representations.

However, quaternionic discrete series representations, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete arithmetic pairings over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, exceptional $E_7$ Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
