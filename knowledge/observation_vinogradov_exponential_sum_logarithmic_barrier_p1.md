## Summary
Even with optimal decoupling inequalities (Bourgain, Demeter, Guth 2016 for the Vinogradov Mean Value Theorem) and Wooley's efficient congruencing, classical exponential sum methods applied to $\zeta'/\zeta$ via the trigonometric positivity $3 + 4\cos\theta + \cos 2\theta \ge 0$ are mathematically restricted to logarithmic zero-free regions of the form:
$$\sigma \ge 1 - \frac{c}{(\log |t|)^{2/3}(\log\log |t|)^{1/3}}$$
The width of this region shrinks to zero as $|t| \to \infty$. Decoupling and exponential sum methods are structurally incapable of penetrating the critical strip to reach any fixed vertical line $\sigma > 1/2 + \delta$, much less the critical line $\sigma = 1/2$. Consequently, bounding prime exponential sums alone cannot serve as an unconditioned forward engine for the Riemann Hypothesis.
