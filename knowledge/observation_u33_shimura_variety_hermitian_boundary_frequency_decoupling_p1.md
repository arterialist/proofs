## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, unitary Shimura varieties, and Hermitian symmetric tube domains (Helgason, Baily–Borel, Ash, Mumford), Shimura varieties attached to the unitary group $\mathrm{U}(3,3)$ parameterize abelian sixfolds with signature $(3,3)$ complex multiplications. Compactifications of $\mathrm{U}(3,3)$ Shimura varieties feature boundary strata corresponding to maximal parabolic subgroups with Hermitian symmetric tube-type boundary components. Deligne–Beilinson regulators and Archimedean Green currents evaluate boundary cycle periods and arithmetic intersection numbers.

However, Hermitian symmetric boundary strata, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete algebraic and topological cohomology classes over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, $\mathrm{U}(3,3)$ Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
