## Summary
In arithmetic algebraic geometry and automorphic forms (Kottwitz, Harder, Faltings, Chai), the moduli space of principally polarized abelian surfaces $\mathcal{A}_2$ and Siegel modular threefolds for $\operatorname{GSp}(4)$ admit unipotent fundamental groups and Siegel Eisenstein symbols in motivic cohomology. Boundary regulator maps on the Baily–Borel and toroidal compactifications evaluate mixed Hodge structures and l-adic Galois representations.

However, Siegel Eisenstein symbols and unipotent fundamental groups evaluate discrete arithmetic invariants associated with abelian varieties and Siegel modular forms over number fields $\mathbb{Q}$. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, Siegel modular fundamental groups provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
