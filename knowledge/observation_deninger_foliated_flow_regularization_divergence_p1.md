## Summary
Christopher Deninger's program aims to realize the non-trivial Riemann zeros as eigenvalues of the Frobenius infinitesimal generator $\Theta$ on an arithmetic cohomology $H^i(\overline{\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}})$, with completed xi expressed as a regularized determinant $\xi(s) = \det_\infty(s \cdot \mathrm{Id} - \Theta)$. However, the underlying geometric space $X$ with a 1D foliated flow whose closed orbits correspond to primes $p$ with period $\log p$ remains completely unconstructed in algebraic or differential topology. Furthermore, regularizing the transverse Lefschetz trace formula over non-compact foliated leaves produces ultraviolet divergences: the distributional trace of $e^{-t\Theta}$ re-encounters the exact poles of $\zeta'/\zeta$, requiring circular zero-freeness assumptions to close the contour across $\operatorname{Re}(s) > 1/2$.
