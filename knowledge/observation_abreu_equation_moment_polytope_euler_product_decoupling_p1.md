## Summary
In toric differential geometry and extremal Kähler metrics (Abreu, Guillemin, Donaldson), Abreu's fully non-linear fourth-order partial differential equation $\sum_{j,k} \partial_j \partial_k (U^{jk}) = -S$ governs the scalar curvature of toric Kähler manifolds via strictly convex symplectic potentials $u(x)$ on Delzant moment polytopes $\Delta \subset \mathbb{R}^n$. Donaldson's boundary value framework establishes existence of constant scalar curvature Kähler metrics from K-stability of toric weak Fano manifolds.

However, boundary value solutions of Abreu's equation on moment polytopes evaluate real convex analysis and fourth-order elliptic PDE solutions on compact Euclidean polytopes $\Delta$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, Abreu equation boundary value solutions provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
