## Summary
Beilinson's conjectures on special values of $L$-functions relate the leading coefficient of $L(M, s)$ at integer points $s = n \in \mathbb{Z}$ to the regulator pairing between motivic cohomology groups $H^i_{\mathcal{M}}(X, \mathbb{Q}(n))$ (or higher Chow groups $CH^p(X, q)$) and Deligne-Beilinson cohomology $H^i_{\mathcal{D}}(X, \mathbb{R}(n))$. When applied to the motivic structure of $\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$, the regulator map yields Borel's regulators and Dirichlet's class number formula / regulator identities at negative odd and positive even integers.

However, motivic cohomology and regulator pairings are strictly discrete algebraic invariants defined only at integer twists $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. They possess zero continuous analytic continuation or interpolation mechanism into the complex plane or along the vertical critical line $s = 1/2 + it$. The pairing is non-trivial exclusively when the algebraic cycle or K-theory class has discrete motivic weight. Consequently, motivic cohomology is completely decoupled from the continuous locus of non-trivial zeros $\rho = 1/2 + it$, providing zero analytical constraint against off-line zeros in the critical strip.
