## Summary
In the geometric Langlands program (Beilinson–Drinfeld, Hitchin, Arinkin, Gaitsgory), geometric Langlands correspondence establishes an equivalence between $D$-modules (Hecke eigensheaves) on the moduli stack of bundles $\operatorname{Bun}_G$ over an algebraic curve $X$ and coherent sheaves on the moduli space of local systems $\operatorname{Loc}_{G^\vee}$. In the classical limit, the cotangent bundle $T^*\operatorname{Bun}_G$ fibers over the Hitchin base $B$ via Hitchin spectral varieties and commuting Hamiltonians.

However, Hitchin spectral curves and geometric Langlands $D$-modules describe algebraic coherent sheaves and flat connections over algebraic curves $X$. These geometric spaces decouple completely from the continuous transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of arithmetic Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Hitchin integrable systems and Hecke eigensheaves provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
