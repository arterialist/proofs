## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, Siegel modular varieties, and boundary cohomology (Harder, Kottwitz, Pink, Faltings–Chai), Shimura varieties attached to the symplectic group $\mathrm{Sp}(4)$ with parahoric level structures admit toroidal compactifications whose boundary strata parameterize degenerating abelian surfaces and semi-abelian varieties. Harder–Kottwitz boundary cohomology vanishing and Deligne–Beilinson regulators evaluate arithmetic cycle classes and Archimedean Green functions on boundary components.

However, parahoric boundary degenerations, Harder–Kottwitz boundary cohomology vanishing, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete topological cohomology and arithmetic intersection numbers over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, $\mathrm{Sp}(4)$ parahoric Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
