## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, automorphic representation theory, and Shimura varieties (Freudenthal, Jacobson, Gross, Savin), exceptional Shimura varieties attached to Cayley Dickson non-compact real forms such as $F_{4(4)}$ and $F_{4(-20)}$ admit automorphic representations and special algebraic cycles within their discrete spectrum. Deligne–Beilinson regulators and Archimedean Green functions evaluate arithmetic intersection pairings and central values of automorphic $L$-functions associated with exceptional octonionic representations.

However, Cayley Dickson real form representations, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete arithmetic pairings over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, exceptional $F_4$ Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
