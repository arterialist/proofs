## Summary

However, Tian's alpha-invariants on Fano complete intersections evaluate algebraic intersection numbers, multi-degrees, and singularity thresholds of algebraic varieties over $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, Fano complete intersection alpha-invariants provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
