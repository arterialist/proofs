## Summary
In arithmetic algebraic geometry and Shimura curves (Gross, Zagier, Zhang, Yuan, Kudla), Shimura curves $X_B$ attached to indefinite quaternion division algebras $B$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ parametrize abelian surfaces with quaternionic multiplication. Special CM points $P_K \in X_B$ associated to imaginary quadratic fields $K$ yield Heegner points in the Jacobians of Shimura curves. The Gross–Zagier–Zhang formula equates the Néron–Tate height $\langle P_K, P_K \rangle_{\mathrm{NT}}$ of a Heegner point to the central derivative $L'(1/2, \pi \times \chi_K)$ of the Rankin–Selberg convolution.

However, Heegner point heights and Gross–Zagier central derivative formulas evaluate algebraic heights of arithmetic cycles over number fields and discrete special values $L'(1/2)$. They operate within the discrete Mordell–Weil theory of abelian varieties and arithmetic intersection theory, which decouples completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, quaternionic Heegner height pairings provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
