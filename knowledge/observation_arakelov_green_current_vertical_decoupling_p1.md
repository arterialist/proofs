## Summary
In Gillet-Soulé Arakelov intersection theory, an arithmetic surface $\mathcal{X} \to \operatorname{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$ is compactified at Archimedean places by equipping the Riemann surface $X_\infty = \mathcal{X}(\mathbb{C})$ with Hermitian metrics and Green currents $g_v$ satisfying the Poisson-type equation $\partial\bar{\partial} g_v + \delta_Z = [\omega_v]$, where $\omega_v$ is a smooth $(1, 1)$-form representing the first Chern class $c_1(\bar{\mathcal{L}})$. The arithmetic intersection pairing $\langle \bar{D}_1, \bar{D}_2 \rangle$ combines non-Archimedean intersection numbers $\sum_{p} (D_1 \cdot D_2)_p \log p$ with the Archimedean Green integral $\frac{1}{2} \int_{X_\infty} g_v(D_1) \delta_{D_2}$.

However, the Archimedean curvature forms $\omega_v$ are smooth differential forms on the compactified Riemann surface that depend only on the Kähler metric; they are completely invariant under, and decoupled from, the vertical continuous phase oscillations $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ along the critical line. Furthermore, the arithmetic Hodge index theorem and Faltings height positivity $\langle \bar{\omega}_{\mathcal{X}/\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}}, \bar{\omega}_{\mathcal{X}/\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}} \rangle \ge 0$ apply to global divisors supported along the boundary or modular cusps. This global geometric positivity provides zero transverse coercivity over individual interior point-mass defects off the critical line ($\beta > 1/2$), leaving off-line zeros unconstrained.
