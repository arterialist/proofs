## Summary
In algebraic geometry and K-stability (Altmann, Hausen, Süß, Ilten), Fano varieties endowed with an effective torus action of complexity $k = \dim(X) - \dim(T) = 4$ are parameterized combinatorially by multi-fan divisorial systems and Cox ring quotients over a 4-dimensional base. K-stability of complexity-4 Fano $T$-varieties reduces to volume minimization and delta invariant computations over high-dimensional polyhedral fans and higher-dimensional convex cones.

However, these high-dimensional polyhedral fan valuations evaluate convex polyhedral cone volumes and birational stability thresholds of algebraic varieties over $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, complexity-4 $T$-variety polyhedral fan invariants provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
