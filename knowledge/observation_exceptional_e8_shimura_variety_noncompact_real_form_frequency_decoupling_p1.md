## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, automorphic representation theory, and Shimura varieties (Borel, Wallach, Gan, Savin, Vogan), exceptional Shimura varieties attached to non-compact real forms such as $E_{8(4)}$ and $E_{8(-24)}$ admit automorphic representations and special algebraic cycles within their discrete spectrum. Deligne–Beilinson regulators and Archimedean Green functions evaluate arithmetic intersection pairings and central values of automorphic $L$-functions associated with exceptional representations.

However, non-compact real form representations, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete arithmetic pairings over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, exceptional $E_8$ Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
