## Summary
In arithmetic and complex algebraic geometry (Kato, Usui, Steenbrink, Deligne), logarithmic Hodge structures and log de Rham cohomology extend classical Hodge theory to degenerating families of algebraic varieties endowed with logarithmic structures along normal crossing divisors. The asymptotic behavior of variations of mixed Hodge structures near singular fibers is governed by the nilpotent monodromy operator $N = \log(T)$ and the associated Deligne-Steenbrink weight filtration $W(N)$.

However, polarized log Hodge structures and nilpotent monodromy operators evaluate discrete combinatorial data (weight filtrations, Picard-Lefschetz monodromy, and asymptotic period mappings) attached to boundary divisors of compactifications over $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, log Hodge monodromy structures provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
