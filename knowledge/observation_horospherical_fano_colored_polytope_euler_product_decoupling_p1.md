## Summary
In spherical geometry and K-stability (Delcroix, Donaldson, Tian, Chi Li, Fujita), horospherical Fano varieties $X$ are equivariant compactifications of homogeneous spaces $G/H$ where $H$ contains a maximal unipotent subgroup $U$. By the Luna–Vust theory and Delcroix's criterion, K-stability of horospherical Fano varieties is equivalent to a barycenter condition for a modified Duistermaat–Heckman measure on a colored moment polytope $P \subset \mathfrak{a}^*$, where the measure is weighted by the Weyl denominator and volume polynomial of the colored fan.

However, colored moment polytopes, modified Duistermaat–Heckman measures, and barycenter conditions evaluate algebraic group representation data, Weyl group root lengths, and convex polytope volumes over $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, horospherical Fano moment polytope invariants provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
