## Summary
In non-commutative algebraic geometry and mirror symmetry (Katzarkov, Kontsevich, Pantev, Tabuada), non-commutative Hodge structures are defined on smooth proper dg-categories or Calabi–Yau $A_\infty$-categories via periodic cyclic homology $HP_\bullet(\mathcal{C})$ and topological $K$-theory. The non-commutative Hodge filtration and semi-infinite variations of Hodge structures endow these categories with abstract polarization pairings $\langle -, - \rangle$.

However, non-commutative Hodge polarizations evaluate abstract geometric periods and pairings on cyclic homology modules. While classifying categorical degenerations and Picard–Fuchs differential equations, these period pairings evaluate integrals of algebraic differential forms on geometric moduli spaces. They do not encode the multiplicative arithmetic of the prime numbers $\{p\}$ or the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, non-commutative Hodge theory provides zero transverse mechanism to establish zero-free regions for $\zeta(s)$ in the critical strip.
