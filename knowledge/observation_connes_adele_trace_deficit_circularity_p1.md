## Summary

However, Connes and Meyer proved that the non-negativity of the trace deficit $D(h * \tilde{h}) \ge 0$ on the space of test functions is mathematically equivalent to the quadratic positivity condition in Weil's explicit formula (Pitfall 023). Thus, establishing the positivity of the adèle class trace deficit carries 100% of Millennium RH analytical depth.
