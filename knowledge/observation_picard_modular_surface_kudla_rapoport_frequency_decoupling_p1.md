## Summary
In arithmetic intersection theory, Shimura varieties, and Kudla's program (Kudla, Rapoport, Yang, Bruinier), Picard modular surfaces attached to unitary groups $\operatorname{PU}(2,1)$ over imaginary quadratic fields $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d})$ admit arithmetic special cycles (Kudla–Rapoport cycles) and Borcherds products. Deligne–Beilinson regulators and Archimedean Green currents evaluate arithmetic intersection numbers and central derivatives of automorphic $L$-functions.

However, Kudla–Rapoport special cycle intersections, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete arithmetic intersection numbers and height pairings over number fields $\mathbb{Q}$. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, Picard modular surfaces provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
