## Summary
In homological mirror symmetry (Kontsevich), the derived Fukaya $A_\infty$-category $D^b\mathcal{F}(X)$ of a symplectic manifold $(X, \omega)$ is equivalent to the derived category of coherent sheaves $D^b\operatorname{Coh}(X^\vee)$ on its mirror complex manifold $X^\vee$. Generating functions of open/closed Gromov–Witten invariants and Floer $A_\infty$-products $\mu^k$ correspond to periods $\int_\gamma \Omega$ satisfying Picard–Fuchs differential equations.

However, homological mirror symmetry relates symplectic topological invariants (Gromov–Witten invariants) to geometric variations of Hodge structures and complex periods on algebraic Calabi–Yau varieties. The solutions to Picard–Fuchs equations represent algebraic or hypergeometric periods that are functions of moduli parameters $q = e^{2\pi i \tau}$, but provide zero analytical control over the distribution of zeros of arithmetic Euler products along the continuous vertical line $s = 1/2 + it$. Symplectic Floer cohomology cannot distinguish between critical-line zeros and hypothetical off-line zeros, failing to provide transverse coercivity.
