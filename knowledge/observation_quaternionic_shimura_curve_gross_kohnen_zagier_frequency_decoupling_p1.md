## Summary
In arithmetic geometry and automorphic forms (Gross, Kohnen, Zagier, Kudla, Rapoport), quaternionic Shimura curves $X_D$ associated to indefinite quaternion division algebras $D$ over totally real number fields $F$ admit special divisors (Heegner points/cycles) whose arithmetic intersection numbers and Néron–Tate heights are captured by coefficients of Jacobi and modular forms. Deligne–Beilinson regulators and Archimedean Green functions evaluate the Archimedean contribution to arithmetic height pairings and central derivatives of automorphic $L$-functions $L'(1/2, f \otimes \chi)$.

However, Gross–Kohnen–Zagier Heegner points, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete arithmetic intersection numbers and height pairings over global number fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, quaternionic Shimura curves provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
