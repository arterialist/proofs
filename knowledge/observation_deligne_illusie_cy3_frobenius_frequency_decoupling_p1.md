## Summary
In arithmetic algebraic geometry and positive characteristic Hodge theory (Deligne, Illusie, Mehta, Ramanathan), a smooth projective variety $X$ over a perfect field of characteristic $p > \dim(X)$ that admits a flat lifting $\widetilde{X}$ to the second Witt vectors $W_2(k)$ satisfies the degeneration of the Hodge–de Rham spectral sequence $E_1^{p, q} \implies H_{\mathrm{dR}}^{p+q}(X/k)$. When applied to Calabi–Yau threefolds, Frobenius splittings and Cartier operators provide splitting homomorphisms $\mathcal{O}_X 	o F_* \mathcal{O}_X$.

However, Frobenius splittings and Cartier operator actions evaluate discrete algebraic Frobenius endomorphisms and local reduction invariants over finite fields $\mathbb{F}_p$. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, Deligne–Illusie Frobenius splitting structures provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
