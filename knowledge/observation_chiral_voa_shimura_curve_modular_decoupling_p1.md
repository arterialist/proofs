## Summary
In chiral conformal field theory and geometric representation theory (Beilinson–Drinfeld, Frenkel–Ben-Zvi, Zhu), chiral algebras and modular vertex operator algebras (VOAs) over algebraic curves $X$ evaluate chiral homology spaces $H_\bullet^{\mathrm{ch}}(X, \mathcal{A})$ and conformal blocks. When evaluated over Shimura curves $X_\Gamma = \Gamma \backslash \mathbb{H}$, the partition functions and correlation functions transform as automorphic forms under arithmetic Fuchsian groups $\Gamma$.

However, chiral correlation functions evaluate automorphic modular forms on the modular curve. While exhibiting modular invariance under the mapping class group and Hecke symmetries, automorphic modularity alone cannot force the non-trivial zeros of associated Dirichlet series onto the critical line $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$, as evidenced by Davenport–Heilbronn modular counterexamples. Chiral VOAs evaluate geometric correlation functions that decouple completely from the continuous transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of individual complex roots of $\zeta(s)$.
