## Summary
In geometric representation theory of reductive groups over finite fields (Deligne, Lusztig), Deligne–Lusztig varieties $X_w$ and character sheaves parameterize irreducible representations and Lusztig's unipotent characters of $G(\mathbb{F}_q)$. The trace of Frobenius on the $l$-adic étale cohomology of character sheaves computes character values via algebraic Lusztig polynomials.

However, these character values are finite algebraic sums over finite algebraic varieties over $\mathbb{F}_q$. They decouple completely from the continuous transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of arithmetic Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Character sheaves on finite groups of Lie type provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate hypothetical complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
