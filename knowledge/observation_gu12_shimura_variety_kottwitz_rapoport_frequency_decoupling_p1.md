## Summary
In arithmetic geometry, PEL type Shimura varieties, and Iwahori level degenerations (Kottwitz, Rapoport, Haines, Ngo), Shimura varieties attached to the unitary similitude group $\mathrm{GU}(1,2)$ over quadratic imaginary fields parameterize polarized Picard surfaces and abelian threefolds with PEL structures. Kottwitz–Rapoport stratifications classify degenerating fibers across Iwahori and parahoric level structures, and Deligne–Beilinson regulators evaluate arithmetic cycle classes and Archimedean Green functions on these strata.

However, Kottwitz–Rapoport stratifications, Deligne–Beilinson regulators, and Archimedean Green functions evaluate discrete algebraic and topological intersection numbers over global fields. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, $\mathrm{GU}(1,2)$ Shimura varieties provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
