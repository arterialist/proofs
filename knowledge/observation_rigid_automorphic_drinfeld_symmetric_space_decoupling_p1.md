## Summary
In rigid analytic geometry and non-Archimedean uniformization (Drinfel'd, Manin, Teitelbaum, Schneider, van der Put), rigid automorphic forms are holomorphic functions on the $p$-adic upper half plane $\Omega = \mathbb{P}^1_{\mathbb{C}_p} \setminus \mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{Q}_p)$ invariant under discrete cocompact subgroups $\Gamma \subset \mathrm{PGL}_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$. Via the Poisson transform, these forms correspond bijectively to harmonic cocycles and finitely additive harmonic measures on the Bruhat–Tits tree $\mathcal{T}_{\mathbb{Q}_p}$.

However, rigid automorphic forms on Drinfel'd symmetric spaces describe discrete $p$-adic spectral decompositions localized at a single non-Archimedean place $p$. These discrete tree representations decouple completely across distinct primes $p \ne \ell$ and lack any continuous Archimedean vertical analytic continuation over the critical strip $s = 1/2 + it$. Bruhat–Tits harmonic measures evaluate discrete building metrics that possess zero transverse coercivity to constrain the root distributions of global complex $L$-functions off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
