## Summary
In geometric representation theory and K-stability (Bott, Samelson, Goresky–Kottwitz–MacPherson, Fiebig, Fujita, Blum), Bott–Samelson varieties $Z(\underline{w}) = P_{s_1} \times^B \dots \times^B P_{s_r} / B$ are smooth projective resolutions of Schubert varieties in flag manifolds $G/B$. Equivariant cohomology $H^*_T(Z(\underline{w}))$ and K-stability thresholds $\delta(Z(\underline{w}))$ are determined by localization to the 1-skeleton of the moment graph (GKM theory), where vertices correspond to subexpressions and edges correspond to torus-invariant rational curves weighted by roots.

However, moment graph 1-skeletons, GKM localization, and valuative invariants of Bott–Samelson varieties evaluate equivariant intersection numbers, Weyl group root hyperplanes, and combinatorial volumes over $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, Bott–Samelson moment graph invariants provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
