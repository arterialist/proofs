## Summary
In birational geometry and K-stability of Fano varieties (Tian, Donaldson, Fujita, Li, Blum, Jonsson, Liu, Xu), the alpha invariant $\alpha(S)$ and delta invariant $\delta(S)$ of del Pezzo surfaces with Du Val quotient singularities characterize uniform K-stability and the existence of orbifold Kähler–Einstein metrics. The Fujita–Li valuative criterion determines Ding and K-stability via the valuative invariant $\beta(E) = A_X(E) - S(E) > 0$ across prime divisors $E$ over $X$.

However, valuative invariants and K-stability of singular del Pezzo surfaces evaluate algebraic log discrepancies $A_X(E)$ and expected vanishing orders $S(E)$ across birational models over $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, singular del Pezzo valuative invariants provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
