## Summary
In arithmetic spin geometry and twistor theory (Atiyah, Singer, Hitchin, Bismut), the Dirac operator $D$ acting on harmonic spinor sections of twistor spin bundles $S^+ \oplus S^-$ computes topological $\widehat{A}$-genera and arithmetic index pairings $\langle D u, v \rangle$. On arithmetic curves, Quillen metrics and holomorphic torsion evaluate geometric and spectral invariants of Riemannian manifolds.

However, these index pairings evaluate classical topological characteristic numbers and finite-dimensional spaces of harmonic spinors. They decouple completely from the continuous transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of arithmetic Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Arithmetic Dirac operators on twistor spaces provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate hypothetical complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
