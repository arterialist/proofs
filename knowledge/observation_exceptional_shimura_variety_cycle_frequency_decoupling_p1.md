## Summary
In arithmetic algebraic geometry and the theory of Shimura varieties (Baily, Borel, Deligne, Milne), exceptional Shimura varieties of type $E_6$ and $E_7$ arise from exceptional algebraic groups and their symmetric domains associated with Freudenthal triple systems and Cayley planes $\mathbb{OP}^2$. Special algebraic cycles on these varieties parametrize sub-Shimura varieties associated with exceptional subgroups and discrete Weyl group orbits.

However, special cycle classes on exceptional Shimura varieties evaluate discrete motivic cohomology classes and algebraic intersection numbers in Chow groups over number fields. They specialize to discrete algebraic periods that decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, exceptional Shimura cycle classes provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
