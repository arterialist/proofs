## Summary
In the K-stability of polarized algebraic varieties (Pasquier, Delcroix, Li, Wang), horospherical varieties are $G$-varieties where a Borel subgroup acts with an open orbit whose stabilizer contains a maximal unipotent subgroup. The existence of Kähler–Einstein metrics and non-Archimedean Ding stability on horospherical varieties is characterized by barycentric volume conditions of moment polytopes with respect to Duistermaat–Heckman measures.

However, horospherical moment polytopes and Duistermaat–Heckman measures evaluate continuous piecewise polynomial measures and convex polyhedral geometry determined by the root system of $G$. They decouple completely from the arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, horospherical geometry and moment polytope stability provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
