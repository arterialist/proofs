# Observation: Modular Converse Non-Coercivity and Davenport-Heilbronn Automorphic Independence

Type: mathematical_observation
Confidence: high
Source Nodes: [explorer_r9_L00_N08, explore_merger_r9_L01_N04, explore_merger_r9_L04_N00]

## Summary
Modular converse theorems (Weil for $\mathrm{GL}(2)$, Cogdell-Piatetski-Shapiro for $\mathrm{GL}(n)$) characterize automorphic representations from analytic continuation, vertical strip boundedness, and functional equations of twist families $L(s \times \chi)$. However, automorphy alone provides zero coercive constraint on zero locations in the critical strip: Davenport-Heilbronn and Epstein zeta functions possess exact functional equations and modular provenance yet have infinitely many off-line zeros (Pitfall 017). Rankin-Selberg non-vanishing is strictly confined to the boundary line $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1$ and cannot penetrate into the critical strip $\operatorname{Re}(s) \in (1/2, 1)$ without unproved Ramanujan-Petersson conjectures or circular zero-freeness assumptions.
