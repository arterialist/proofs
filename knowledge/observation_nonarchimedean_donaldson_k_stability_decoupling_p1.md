## Summary
In complex algebraic geometry and pluripotential theory (Donaldson, Tian, Chen–Donaldson–Sun, Boucksom–Jonsson), K-stability and Ding stability of Fano or polarized varieties characterize the existence of Kähler–Einstein and constant scalar curvature metrics via test configurations $(\mathcal{X}, \mathcal{L})$. Over non-Archimedean fields, Donaldson's functional and the non-Archimedean Mabuchi energy evaluate the non-Archimedean Futaki invariant $\operatorname{Fut}(\mathcal{X}, \mathcal{L})$.

However, non-Archimedean Donaldson functionals and K-stability characterize the algebraic geometry of degenerating projective varieties over a discretely valued field. The Futaki invariant and Ding functional evaluate intersection numbers of line bundles on test configurations, which describe algebraic stability of geometric varieties. These invariants decouple completely from the multiplicative distribution of prime numbers $\{p\}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, non-Archimedean K-stability provides zero transverse mechanism to establish zero-free regions for $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
