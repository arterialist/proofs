## Summary

$$\sum_{\lambda_k \in \Omega} g_\Omega(z_0, \lambda_k) = \int_{\partial \Omega} \log |f(\zeta)| \, \frac{\partial g_\Omega}{\partial n}(\zeta, z_0) \, |d\zeta| + \mathcal{R}_\infty - \log |f(z_0)|$$

If an off-line zero $\lambda_0 \in \Omega$ is introduced, the boundary integral of the corresponding Hadamard factor $\log |1 - \zeta/\lambda_0|$ evaluated via harmonic measure automatically shifts by exactly $+g_\Omega(z_0, \lambda_0)$. Consequently, potential-theoretic balance equations cannot force the Green defect sum $\sum g_\Omega = 0$ or exclude off-line zeros without an independent, external evaluation of the boundary integral, which is circular with the Riemann Hypothesis itself ($N_0(T) = N(T)$).
