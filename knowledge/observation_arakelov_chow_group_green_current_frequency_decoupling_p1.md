## Summary
In arithmetic intersection theory and Arakelov geometry (Arakelov, Gillet–Soulé, Faltings, Deligne), arithmetic surfaces $\mathcal{X}_{/\mathcal{O}_K}$ and Shimura curves are studied via arithmetic Chow groups $\widehat{\operatorname{CH}}^1(X)$, Deligne–Beilinson regulators $r_{\mathcal{D}}$, Archimedean Green currents $g_v$, and Néron–Tate height pairings. Archimedean contributions are governed by smooth differential forms and Green functions satisfying Laplace-type equations on Riemann surfaces.

However, smooth Archimedean Green currents and discrete arithmetic intersection pairings evaluate global height pairings over number fields $\mathbb{Q}$. They decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, Shimura curve Arakelov Chow groups provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
