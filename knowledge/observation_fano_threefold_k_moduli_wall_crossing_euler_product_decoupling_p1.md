## Summary
In algebraic geometry and K-stability (Mori, Mukai, Donaldson, Tian, Chi Li, Xu, Liu, Ascher, Bejleri, Blum), smooth Fano threefolds are classified into 105 deformation families, with 98 families having Picard rank $\rho(X) \ge 2$. Across the space of polarization classes and anticanonical divisors, K-moduli spaces $M^K(X)$ undergo wall-crossing phenomena governed by the stability threshold $\delta(X)$ and valuative $\beta$-invariants $\beta_X(E) = A_X(E) - S_X(E)$.

However, K-moduli wall-crossing and valuative invariants of Fano threefolds evaluate intersection numbers, log canonical thresholds, and volumes of algebraic divisors over $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, Fano threefold K-moduli wall crossing provides zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
