## Summary

It characterizes the algebraic stability of geometric varieties under degenerations. This threshold invariant decouples completely from the multiplicative structure of the prime numbers $\{p\}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, valuative Ding stability and delta invariants provide zero transverse mechanism to eliminate complex roots of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
