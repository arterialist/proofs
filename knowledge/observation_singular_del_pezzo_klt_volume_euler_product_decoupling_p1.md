## Summary
In birational geometry and K-stability (Chi Li, Tian, Cheltsov, Fujita, Blum, Liu, Xu), K-stability on log del Pezzo surfaces $(S, \Delta)$ with non-rational or Kawamata log terminal (klt) singularities is characterized by local normalized volume minimization $\widehat{\operatorname{vol}}(v) = A_{(S, \Delta)}(v)^2 \cdot \operatorname{vol}(v)$ over the valuative spectrum $\operatorname{Val}_{S, x}$. Stability thresholds $\delta(S, \Delta) > 1$ and Tian's $\alpha$-invariants govern the existence of singular Kähler–Einstein metrics and K-moduli compactifications.

However, normalized volumes of klt singularities and valuative stability invariants evaluate local birational invariants of exceptional divisor valuations, log discrepancies, and volumes of algebraic singularities over $\mathbb{C}$. They decouple completely from the discrete arithmetic prime distribution over $\mathbb{Z}$ and the infinite Euler product $\prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}$. Consequently, singular del Pezzo klt volume minimizers provide zero transverse analytical mechanism to detect or eliminate off-line complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$.
