## Summary
In representation theory and arithmetic algebraic geometry (Deligne, Lusztig, Digne, Michel), Deligne–Lusztig varieties $X(w)$ attached to reductive groups over finite fields $\mathbb{F}_q$ produce representations of finite groups of Lie type via the $\ell$-adic cohomology $H_c^i(X(w), \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$. The eigenvalues of Frobenius acting on these cohomology spaces are algebraic integers with weights governed by Deligne's purity theorems.

However, Frobenius eigenvalues and Lusztig character sheaves evaluate finite-field character formulas and étale cohomology over algebraic closures. They specialize to discrete algebraic eigenvalues that decouple completely from the continuous vertical transcendental frequencies $t = \operatorname{Im}(s)$ of Dirichlet series $\zeta(s) = \sum n^{-s}$. Consequently, Deligne–Lusztig Frobenius weight structures provide zero transverse analytical coercivity to constrain or eliminate the complex zeros of $\zeta(s)$ off $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
