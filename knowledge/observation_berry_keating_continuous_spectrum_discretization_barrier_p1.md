## Summary

1. Break self-adjointness, producing complex eigenvalues off the real line $\mathbb{R}$; or
2. Introduce energy-dependent phase shifts $\theta(E)$ that circularly encode the Riemann zeta zeros $\theta(E) = \arg \Xi(1/2 + iE)$ by assumption.
