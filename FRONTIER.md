# Current RH frontier

Counting advances by $n\mapsto n+1$; factorization records which prime generators and composite histories have appeared. The results here test whether that joint structure can control the signed prime-power error at the scale needed by the Riemann hypothesis. None proves RH.

The strongest zero-side statement in this repository is the [fixed-bump Weil microcluster bound](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/actual-weil-offline-microcluster-mass.md). It limits the mass of off-line zeros in a specified annulus and gives a bound on central clusters. It still permits an isolated off-line zero. The [annular Sobolev sampling bound](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/actual-annulus-sobolev-zero-sampling.md) makes the complete Weil Gram positive outside a very small exceptional carrier set; that is an almost-everywhere statement, not global positivity.

The [chirped packet construction](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/chirped-brun-titchmarsh-span-constellation.md) proves positive complete Weil forms on a growing finite family of packets. Its selected off-line zero contribution is too small to force a contradiction. On the arithmetic side, the [reciprocal-prime estimate](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/actual-reciprocal-prime-high-cofactor-saving.md) saves a power on one high-cofactor range, while the [proper-cofactor dual Dirichlet cancellation](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/actual-proper-cofactor-dirichlet-dual-cancellation.md) proves that the remaining proper cofactors $k \in [2, T^{1-\delta}]$ factor as $k^{-\rho} J(\rho, T)$ and sum to a microscopic dual Dirichlet polynomial of length $T^\delta / (2\pi)$, cancelling the lead zero-side pole of the prime row and removing the termwise $O(H/\sqrt N)$ barrier. Furthermore, the [dual Dirichlet offline decay theorem](unique-contributions/exact-dual-dirichlet-offline-decay-and-power-suppression-threshold.md) (machine-verified in [`DualDirichletOfflineDecay.lean`](formalization/BuildingBlocks/DualDirichletOfflineDecay.lean)) proves that for every off-line zero candidate $\beta = 1/2 + \varepsilon$ ($\varepsilon > 0$), the short dual Dirichlet sum multiplied by the scattering multiplier $\chi(\rho)$ satisfies an unconditional power decay $O(T^{-\kappa})$ with $\kappa = \varepsilon(1-\delta) - \delta/2 > 0$ whenever $\delta < \varepsilon/\beta$. The underlying algebraic decomposition is compiled in [`ActualCofactorDirichletDualAlgebra.lean`](formalization/BuildingBlocks/ActualCofactorDirichletDualAlgebra.lean). In addition, the [exact proper-cofactor spectral neutralization theorem](unique-contributions/exact-proper-cofactor-spectral-neutralization-and-margin-dominance.md) (machine-verified in [`ActualCofactorSpectralNeutralization.lean`](formalization/BuildingBlocks/ActualCofactorSpectralNeutralization.lean)) proves that the scattering multiplier $\chi(\rho)$ exactly neutralizes the raw off-line zero amplitude ($T^\varepsilon \cdot T^{-\varepsilon} = 1$), depressing the compensated spectral exponent below any pre-assigned $\nu > 0$, ensuring asymptotic dominance of the positive Weil margin $a_\phi \log T$ over any fixed constant bound on the neutralized off-line mass.

**Remaining frontier challenge.** With the off-line dual Dirichlet remainder power-suppressed and exactly neutralized, the remaining obstacle on the arithmetic side is the critical-line background zeros ($\beta = 1/2$, where the exponent is $+\delta/2$ and oscillatory square-root cancellation rather than multiplier decay is required) and establishing the spectral-arithmetic global positivity across all continuous families of test packets simultaneously. None of these estimates yet proves RH.

Analytic estimates in the linked notes are written proofs unless their text names a Lean module. Lean verification of finite identities does not certify the analytic estimates that use them.
