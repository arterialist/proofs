# Current RH frontier

Counting advances by $n\mapsto n+1$; factorization records which prime generators and composite histories have appeared. The results here test whether that joint structure can control the signed prime-power error at the scale needed by the Riemann hypothesis. None proves RH.

The strongest zero-side statement in this repository is the [fixed-bump Weil microcluster bound](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/actual-weil-offline-microcluster-mass.md). It limits the mass of off-line zeros in a specified annulus and gives a bound on central clusters. It still permits an isolated off-line zero. The [annular Sobolev sampling bound](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/actual-annulus-sobolev-zero-sampling.md) makes the complete Weil Gram positive outside a very small exceptional carrier set; that is an almost-everywhere statement, not global positivity.

The [chirped packet construction](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/chirped-brun-titchmarsh-span-constellation.md) proves positive complete Weil forms on a growing finite family of packets. Its selected off-line zero contribution is too small to force a contradiction. On the arithmetic side, the [reciprocal-prime estimate](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/actual-reciprocal-prime-high-cofactor-saving.md) saves a power on one high-cofactor range, while the [proper-cofactor dual Dirichlet cancellation](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/actual-proper-cofactor-dirichlet-dual-cancellation.md) proves that the remaining proper cofactors $k \in [2, T^{1-\delta}]$ factor as $k^{-\rho} J(\rho, T)$ and sum to a microscopic dual Dirichlet polynomial of length $T^\delta / (2\pi)$, cancelling the lead zero-side pole of the prime row and removing the termwise $O(H/\sqrt N)$ barrier. Furthermore, the [dual Dirichlet offline decay theorem](unique-contributions/exact-dual-dirichlet-offline-decay-and-power-suppression-threshold.md) (machine-verified in [`DualDirichletOfflineDecay.lean`](formalization/BuildingBlocks/DualDirichletOfflineDecay.lean)) proves that for every off-line zero candidate $\beta = 1/2 + \varepsilon$ ($\varepsilon > 0$), the short dual Dirichlet sum multiplied by the scattering multiplier $\chi(\rho)$ satisfies an unconditional power decay $O(T^{-\kappa})$ with $\kappa = \varepsilon(1-\delta) - \delta/2 > 0$ whenever $\delta < \varepsilon/\beta$.

**Remaining frontier challenge.** With the off-line dual Dirichlet remainder power-suppressed, the remaining obstacle on the arithmetic side is the critical-line background zeros ($\beta = 1/2$, where the exponent is $+\delta/2$ and oscillatory square-root cancellation rather than multiplier decay is required) and establishing that the implicit constants in the off-line power suppression do not exceed the positive complete-form margin $a_\phi H$ as $T \to \infty$. None of these estimates yet proves RH.

Analytic estimates in the linked notes are written proofs unless their text names a Lean module. Lean verification of finite identities does not certify the analytic estimates that use them.
