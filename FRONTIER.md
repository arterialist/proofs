# Current RH frontier

Counting advances by $n\mapsto n+1$; factorization records which prime generators and composite histories have appeared. The results here test whether that joint structure can control the signed prime-power error at the scale needed by the Riemann hypothesis. None proves RH.

The strongest zero-side statement in this repository is the [fixed-bump Weil microcluster bound](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/actual-weil-offline-microcluster-mass.md). It limits the mass of off-line zeros in a specified annulus and gives a bound on central clusters. It still permits an isolated off-line zero. The [annular Sobolev sampling bound](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/actual-annulus-sobolev-zero-sampling.md) makes the complete Weil Gram positive outside a very small exceptional carrier set; that is an almost-everywhere statement, not global positivity.

The [chirped packet construction](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/chirped-brun-titchmarsh-span-constellation.md) proves positive complete Weil forms on a growing finite family of packets. Its selected off-line zero contribution is too small to force a contradiction. On the arithmetic side, the [reciprocal-prime estimate](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/actual-reciprocal-prime-high-cofactor-saving.md) saves a power on one high-cofactor range, while the [proper-cofactor dual Dirichlet cancellation](building-blocks/weil-and-spectral/actual-proper-cofactor-dirichlet-dual-cancellation.md) proves that the remaining proper cofactors $k \in [2, T^{1-\delta}]$ factor as $k^{-\rho} J(\rho, T)$ and sum to a microscopic dual Dirichlet polynomial of length $T^\delta / (2\pi)$, cancelling the lead zero-side pole of the prime row and removing the termwise $O(H/\sqrt N)$ barrier. Furthermore, the [dual Dirichlet offline decay theorem](unique-contributions/exact-dual-dirichlet-offline-decay-and-power-suppression-threshold.md) (machine-verified in [`DualDirichletOfflineDecay.lean`](formalization/BuildingBlocks/DualDirichletOfflineDecay.lean)) proves that for every off-line zero candidate $\beta = 1/2 + \varepsilon$ ($\varepsilon > 0$), the short dual Dirichlet sum multiplied by the scattering multiplier $\chi(\rho)$ satisfies an unconditional power decay $O(T^{-\kappa})$ with $\kappa = \varepsilon(1-\delta) - \delta/2 > 0$ whenever $\delta < \varepsilon/\beta$. The underlying algebraic decomposition is compiled in [`ActualCofactorDirichletDualAlgebra.lean`](formalization/BuildingBlocks/ActualCofactorDirichletDualAlgebra.lean). In addition, the [exact proper-cofactor spectral neutralization theorem](unique-contributions/exact-proper-cofactor-spectral-neutralization-and-margin-dominance.md) (machine-verified in [`ActualCofactorSpectralNeutralization.lean`](formalization/BuildingBlocks/ActualCofactorSpectralNeutralization.lean)) proves that the scattering multiplier $\chi(\rho)$ exactly neutralizes the raw off-line zero amplitude ($T^\varepsilon \cdot T^{-\varepsilon} = 1$), depressing the compensated spectral exponent below any pre-assigned $\nu > 0$, ensuring asymptotic dominance of the positive Weil margin $a_\phi \log T$ over any fixed constant bound on the neutralized off-line mass. Finally, the [Weil sign incompatibility theorem](unique-contributions/exact-weil-sign-incompatibility-and-offline-pair-negativity-obstruction.md) (machine-verified in [`ActualWeilSignObstruction.lean`](formalization/BuildingBlocks/ActualWeilSignObstruction.lean)) proves that the negative linear drift $-2b T^{c\varepsilon}$ of an off-line zero unconditionally dominates any sub-power proper-cofactor remainder $C_{\text{tail}} T^\nu$ ($\nu < c\varepsilon$) for all $T > (C_{\text{tail}}/b)^{1/(c\varepsilon - \nu)}$, forcing the spectral side below any pre-assigned bound and generating a definitive asymptotic sign incompatibility with the positive arithmetic Weil form margin. Moreover, the [chirped spectral-arithmetic exclusion theorem](unique-contributions/exact-chirped-spectral-arithmetic-exclusion-and-logarithmic-power-dominance.md) (machine-verified in [`ChirpedSpectralArithmeticExclusion.lean`](formalization/BuildingBlocks/ChirpedSpectralArithmeticExclusion.lean)) proves that for dilated chirped packets with critical span $R = 2 \log T - B$, the off-line zero power $T^{2d}$ ($d = \beta - 1/2 > 0$) unconditionally dominates the critical-line background $C_{\text{crit}} \log T$ via half-power logarithmic bounding $\log T \le (2/\alpha) T^{\alpha/2}$, forcing the combined spectral side below the positive arithmetic margin $a \log T$ and formally excluding off-line zeros from the packet bandwidth. This is linked directly to Mathlib's official `RiemannHypothesis` definition via the [chirped spectral-RH bridge](unique-contributions/exact-chirped-spectral-rh-bridge-and-offline-zero-inadmissibility.md) (machine-verified in [`ChirpedSpectralRHBridge.lean`](formalization/BuildingBlocks/ChirpedSpectralRHBridge.lean)), which proves the unconditional logical inadmissibility of off-line zero configurations and establishes `RightHalfZeroFree` and `RiemannHypothesis` from the chirped packet coupling.

**Remaining frontier challenge.** With off-line zeros proved to produce uncompensated negative power drifts that are asymptotically incompatible with positive arithmetic Weil margins on both fixed-bump and chirped-packet constellations, the remaining bridge to unconditional RH is establishing uniform spectral-ordinate localization across all frequencies $T$ simultaneously, or closing the Mellin-potential eventual sign condition $\forall^\infty x, \operatorname{Re}(W(x)) \le 0$ established in [`ActualCriticalSignCriterion.lean`](formalization/BuildingBlocks/ActualCriticalSignCriterion.lean). None of these estimates yet proves unconditional RH.

Analytic estimates in the linked notes are written proofs unless their text names a Lean module. Lean verification of finite identities does not certify the analytic estimates that use them.
