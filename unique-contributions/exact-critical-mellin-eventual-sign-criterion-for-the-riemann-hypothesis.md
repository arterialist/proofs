## 2. Mathematical Proof Architecture

### 2.1. Decomposition of the Transform
The critical Mellin kernel $W(x)$ satisfies:
\[
\mathcal{M}(W)(-s - 1) = \Phi(s) \quad \text{for } \operatorname{Re}(s) > 1,
\]
where $\Phi(s)$ is the filled transform of the logarithmic derivative of $\zeta(s)$ with all trivial poles removed.
Splitting $W$ at $x = A$:
\[
W(x) = W_{\text{init}, A}(x) + W_{\text{tail}, A}(x),
\]
where $W_{\text{init}, A}$ is supported on $[1, A]$ and $W_{\text{tail}, A}$ is supported on $(A, \infty)$.
Under the premise $\operatorname{Re}(W(x)) \le 0$ on $(A, \infty)$, $-W_{\text{tail}, A}$ defines a non-negative Radon measure on $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$.

### 2.2. Landau Abscissa Barrier
By Contribution 136 (`ActualCriticalConvergence`), the positive Laplace measure generated by $-W_{\text{tail}, A}$ has convergence abscissa $\sigma_c \le 1/2$.
Consequently, the Laplace transform converges absolutely and defines a holomorphic function on the open half-plane $\operatorname{Re}(s) > 1/2$.

### 2.3. Zero-Free Region of $\zeta(s)$
The analytic continuation of $\Phi(s)$ onto $\operatorname{Re}(s) > 1/2$ is given by:
\[
\Phi(s) = \mathcal{M}(W_{\text{init}, A})(-s-1) - \int_0^\infty e^{-st} d\mu_A(t).
\]
Since the right-hand side is holomorphic on $\operatorname{Re}(s) > 1/2$, $\Phi(s)$ has no poles in $\{s \in \mathbb{C} \mid \operatorname{Re}(s) > 1/2\}$.
Because the poles of $\Phi(s)$ in the critical strip correspond exactly to the non-trivial zeros $\rho$ of $\zeta(s)$ with residue equal to their multiplicity $m_\rho \ge 1$, the absence of poles on $\operatorname{Re}(s) > 1/2$ implies that $\zeta(s)$ has no zeros with $\operatorname{Re}(\rho) > 1/2$.
By the functional equation $\zeta(s) = 2^s \pi^{s-1} \sin(\pi s/2) \Gamma(1-s) \zeta(1-s)$, all non-trivial zeros must satisfy $\operatorname{Re}(\rho) = 1/2$.
