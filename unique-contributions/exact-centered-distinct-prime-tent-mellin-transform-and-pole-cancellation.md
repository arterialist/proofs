## 1. Executive Summary and Mathematical Statement

The prime-pair correlation and its associated tent function $\sum_{mn \le x} (x - mn)\Lambda(m)\Lambda(n)$ possesses a double pole at $s=1$ generated by $(-\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s))^2 \sim (s-1)^{-2}$. In establishing positivity and non-trivial bounds, the principal pole must be rigorously cancelled, and the diagonal prime-power terms where $m$ and $n$ share a base prime must be subtracted without introducing artificial convergence assumptions.

This contribution proves:

1. **Exact Decomposition of the Actual Centered Tent:**  
   The actual centered distinct-prime cutoff sum $V(x)$ decomposes into the full centered square $N(x)$ minus the same-prime diagonal $T(x)$:
   \[
   V(x) = N(x) - T(x),
   \]
   where $N(x) = \sum_{mn \le x} (x - mn)\Lambda(m)\Lambda(n) - 2 \text{etaSum}(x) + B(x)$, and $T(x) = \sum_{p^j p^k \le x} (x - p^{j+k})\log^2 p$.
2. **Explicit Real Arithmetic Cutoff Representation:**  
   For all $x \ge 0$, $V(x)$ is given by the finite real sum:
   \[
   V(x) = \sum_{n \le x} \left[ (x - n)\Lambda_{\text{distinct}, 2}(n) - \Lambda(n)\frac{x^2 - n^2}{n} \right] + B(x),
   \]
   where $\Lambda_{\text{distinct}, 2}(n) = \sum_{d \mid n, \gcd(\text{rad}(d), \text{rad}(n/d)) = 1} \Lambda(d)\Lambda(n/d)$.
3. **Exact Regularized Mellin Transform:**  
   On the absolute convergence half-plane $\text{Re}(s) > 1$, the Mellin transform of $V(x)$ is identically equal to:
   \[
   \mathcal{M}(V)(-s-1) = \frac{\left(-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)} - \frac{1}{s-1}\right)^2 - H(s)}{s(s+1)},
   \]
   where $H(s) = \sum_p \frac{\log^2 p}{(p^s - 1)^2}$ is the exact same-prime Dirichlet series.
   The subtraction of $2\,\text{etaSum}(x)$ and the baseline $B(x)$ **strictly cancels the first- and second-order poles at $s=1$**, yielding the centered logarithmic derivative squared $\left(-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta} - \frac{1}{s-1}\right)^2$.

## 2. Mathematical Proof

### 2.1. Cutoff Sum Decomposition
By `DistinctPrimeRieszMellin.distinctPair_eq`, the distinct-prime convolution pair is identically the full convolution pair minus the same-prime diagonal pair:
\[
\text{distinctPair} = \text{fullPair} - \text{samePair}.
\]
By linearity of the Riesz cutoff sum $\sum (x - n) a(n)$:
\[
\text{cutoffSum}(\text{distinctPair}, x) = \text{cutoffSum}(\text{fullPair}, x) - \text{cutoffSum}(\text{samePair}, x).
\]
Substituting into $V(x) = \text{cutoffSum}(\text{distinctPair}, x) - 2\,\text{etaSum}(x) + B(x)$ yields $V(x) = N(x) - T(x)$.

### 2.2. Real Arithmetic Cutoff Representation
Using `etaSum_eq_Icc`, the eta cross term expands as:
\[
2\,\text{etaSum}(x) = \sum_{n \le x} \Lambda(n) \frac{x^2 - n^2}{n}.
\]
Combining this with $\text{cutoffSum}(\text{distinctPair}, x) = \sum_{n \le x} (x - n)\Lambda_{\text{distinct}, 2}(n)$ yields the real cutoff formula.

### 2.3. Mellin Transform and Pole Cancellation
From `DistinctPrimeRieszMellin.hasMellin_distinctPrime_cutoffSum`:
\[
\mathcal{M}(\text{cutoffSum}(\text{distinctPair}))(-s-1) = \frac{\left(-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}\right)^2 - H(s)}{s(s+1)}.
\]
From `EtaRieszDirichlet.hasMellin_crossTerm`:
\[
\mathcal{M}(\text{etaSum})(-s-1) = \frac{-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}}{(s-1)(s+1)}.
\]
From `EtaBaselineMellin.hasMellin_B`:
\[
\mathcal{M}(B)(-s-1) = \frac{1}{s(s+1)(s-1)^2}.
\]
Combining these transforms with coefficients $1, -2, 1$:
\[
\mathcal{M}(V)(-s-1) = \frac{\left(-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}\right)^2 - H(s)}{s(s+1)} - \frac{2\left(-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}\right)}{(s-1)(s+1)} + \frac{1}{s(s+1)(s-1)^2}.
\]
Putting everything over the common denominator $s(s+1)$:
\[
\frac{1}{s(s+1)} \left[ \left(-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}\right)^2 - \frac{2s}{s-1}\left(-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}\right) + \frac{1}{(s-1)^2} - H(s) \right].
\]
Using $\frac{2s}{s-1} = 2 + \frac{2}{s-1}$:
\[
\left(-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}\right)^2 - 2\left(-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}\right)\frac{1}{s-1} + \frac{1}{(s-1)^2} = \left(-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)} - \frac{1}{s-1}\right)^2.
\]
Thus:
\[
\mathcal{M}(V)(-s-1) = \frac{\left(-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)} - \frac{1}{s-1}\right)^2 - H(s)}{s(s+1)}.
\]
The singularity at $s=1$ is regularized into an entire expression on $\text{Re}(s) > 1$.
