# Original mathematical contributions

Original results with complete proofs, attribution, and a comparison with the closest prior mathematics.

- [Actual shifted-multiplier sign criterion for RH](actual-shifted-multiplier-sign-criterion.md). A compiled conditional criterion with one explicit, still unproved arithmetic sign premise and a comparison to prior sign criteria.
- [Exact-horizon signed smooth Möbius energy](exact-horizon-signed-friable-energy.md). A written analytic lower bound at the fixed $c(\log N)^2$ cutoff and an RH-equivalent signed transport criterion. Its originality classification is provisional after a bounded primary-literature search; the transport sign remains open.
- [Twice-compensated proper-cone sign theorem](twice-compensated-proper-cone-sign-theorem.md). A proved sign-change barrier for every integrable divisor-cone profile with two vanishing Mellin moments; its specific formulation has not been located in a targeted literature search. It gives no RH-scale bound.
