## 1. Executive Summary and Mathematical Statement

In the compound Poisson and pure jump semigroup formulation of prime arrival clocks, the support of the jump intensity governs the support of the transition laws at all operational times $u \ge 0$. Because the smallest prime is $p = 2$, no prime clock can produce jumps below $\log 2 \approx 0.6931$.

This contribution proves:

1. **Universal Prime Seed Lower Inactivity:**  
   For every prime $p \ge 2$ and every age $v < \log 2$:
   $$
   \text{seed}_{\log p}(v) = 0.
   $$
2. **Exact Initial Gap of the All-Prime Lévy Measure:**  
   The aggregated all-prime Lévy measure $\nu_{\mathcal{P}}$ assigns measure zero to the entire half-line $(-\infty, \log 2)$:
   $$
   \nu_{\mathcal{P}}((-\infty, \log 2)) = 0.
   $$
   In particular, $\nu_{\mathcal{P}}((-\infty, 0)) = 0$, meaning that all jumps are almost everywhere strictly positive: $v \ge \log 2 > 0$ almost everywhere under $\nu_{\mathcal{P}}$.
3. **Convolution Power Support Growth Identity:**  
   For any two finite measures $\mu, \nu$ vanishing on $(-\infty, a)$ and $(-\infty, b)$:
   $$
   (\mu * \nu)((-\infty, a + b)) = 0.
   $$
   By induction, the $n$-fold convolution power $\mu^{*n}$ satisfies:
   $$
   \mu^{*n}((-\infty, n L)) = 0 \quad \text{for every } n \in \mathbb{N}.
   $$
4. **Sub-Threshold Atomicity and Initial Cell Law:**  
   For any compound Poisson law $\mathcal{P}_u = e^{-u \|\mu\|} \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{u^n}{n!} \mu^{*n}$, all terms with $n \ge 1$ have support in $[nL, \infty) \subseteq [L, \infty)$. Therefore, on ANY measurable subset $A \subseteq (-\infty, L)$:
   $$
   \mathcal{P}_u(A) = e^{-u \|\mu\|} \delta_0(A).
   $$
   In particular, on the initial cell $(-\infty, L)$:
   $$
   \mathcal{P}_u((-\infty, L)) = e^{-u \|\mu\|}.
   $$
5. **Exact All-Prime Initial Cell and Zero-Negative-Age Laws:**  
   For the all-prime process $X_u$ generated by $\nu_{\mathcal{P}}$, on the fundamental interval $(-\infty, \log 2)$:
   $$
   \mathbb{P}(X_u \in (-\infty, \log 2)) = \mathbb{P}(X_u = 0) = e^{-u \|\nu_{\mathcal{P}}\|},
   $$
   and the probability of ever visiting a negative age is identically zero:
   $$
   \mathbb{P}(X_u < 0) = 0 \quad \text{for all } u \ge 0.
   $$
   Strict causality is preserved under all convolution powers and continuous operational time evolutions.

## 2. Mathematical Proof

### 2.1. Seed Lower Support Bound
For any prime $p$, $p \ge 2$, hence $\log p \ge \log 2$.
If $v < \log 2$, then $v < \log p$. By `seed_eq_zero`, $\text{seed}_{\log p}(v) = 0$.
Integrating $\text{levyDensity}_S(v) = \sum_{p \in S} \frac{\text{seed}_{\log p}(v)}{v} = 0$ on $(-\infty, \log 2)$ yields $\nu_S((-\infty, \log 2)) = 0$.
Summing over the exhaustion of primes proves $\nu_{\mathcal{P}}((-\infty, \log 2)) = 0$.

### 2.2. Convolution Support Propagation
Let $z = (x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^2$ satisfy $x + y < a + b$.
If $x \ge a$, then $y < (a + b) - x \le b$.
Thus the preimage $\{ (x, y) : x + y < a + b \}$ is covered by $(-\infty, a) \times \mathbb{R} \cup \mathbb{R} \times (-\infty, b)$.
By union bound:
$$
(\mu * \nu)((-\infty, a+b)) \le \mu((-\infty, a)) \nu(\mathbb{R}) + \mu(\mathbb{R}) \nu((-\infty, b)) = 0 + 0 = 0.
$$
By induction, $\mu^{*n}((-\infty, nL)) = 0$.

### 2.3. Initial Region Compound Poisson Distribution
Let $A \subseteq (-\infty, L)$.
For $n \ge 1$, $n L \ge L$, so $A \subseteq (-\infty, L) \subseteq (-\infty, nL)$.
Hence $\mu^{*n}(A) \le \mu^{*n}((-\infty, nL)) = 0$.
In the series $\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{u^n}{n!} \mu^{*n}(A)$, every term for $n \ge 1$ vanishes identically.
Only the $n = 0$ term remains:
$$
\mathcal{P}_u(A) = e^{-u \|\mu\|} \mu^{*0}(A) = e^{-u \|\mu\|} \delta_0(A).
$$
Setting $A = \{0\}$ gives the atom mass $e^{-u \|\mu\|}$.
Setting $A = (-\infty, 0)$ gives $\delta_0((-\infty, 0)) = 0$, so $\mathcal{P}_u((-\infty, 0)) = 0$.
