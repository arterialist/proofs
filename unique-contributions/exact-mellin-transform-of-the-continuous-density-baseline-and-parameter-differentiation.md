## 1. Executive Summary and Mathematical Statement

The continuous background density in prime distribution analysis produces the deterministic baseline $B(x) = \left(\frac{x^2}{2}\log x + \frac{x^2}{4} - \frac{1}{4}\right)\mathbf{1}_{x > 1}$, which cancels the principal pole divergences at $s=1$ in the prime-pair autocorrelation. Because $B(x)$ contains a logarithmic factor $\log x$, computing its Mellin transform requires rigorous justification of differentiation under the improper integral.

This contribution proves:

1. **Exact Step and Logarithmic Step Mellin Transforms:**  
   For the Heaviside step function $H_1(x) = \mathbf{1}_{x > 1}$ and for any $\text{Re}(s) < 0$:
   $$
   \mathcal{M}(H_1)(s) = -\frac{1}{s}.
   $$
   Furthermore, using locally dominated parameter differentiation of the Mellin transform (`mellin_hasDerivAt_of_isBigO_rpow`):
   $$
   \mathcal{M}(\log x \cdot H_1(x))(s) = \frac{d}{ds}\left( -\frac{1}{s} \right) = \frac{1}{s^2}.
   $$
2. **Exact Shifted Power Transforms:**  
   For any $a \in \mathbb{C}$ with $\text{Re}(s + a) < 0$:
   $$
   \mathcal{M}(x^a H_1(x))(s) = -\frac{1}{s+a}, \quad \mathcal{M}(x^a \log x H_1(x))(s) = \frac{1}{(s+a)^2}.
   $$
3. **Exact Closed-Form Rational Mellin Transform of the Full Baseline:**  
   The baseline function $B(x) = \left(\frac{x^2}{2}\log x + \frac{x^2}{4} - \frac{1}{4}\right)\mathbf{1}_{x > 1}$ has the exact continuous Mellin transform on $\text{Re}(s) > 1$:
   $$
   \mathcal{M}(B)(-s-1) = \frac{s}{(s-1)^2 (s+1)}.
   $$
   This rational function provides the exact counter-terms matching the double and simple poles at $s=1$ generated by $(-\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s))^2$ and $-2\,\text{crossTerm}(x)$.

## 2. Mathematical Proof

### 2.1. Step and Logarithmic Moment Transforms
For $\text{Re}(s) < 0$:
$$
\mathcal{M}(H_1)(s) = \int_1^\infty x^{s-1} dx = \left[ \frac{x^s}{s} \right]_1^\infty = -\frac{1}{s}.
$$
To differentiate with respect to $s$, notice that $H_1(x)$ is locally integrable on $(0, \infty)$, $H_1(x) = O(1)$ as $x \to \infty$, and $H_1(x) = 0$ near $0$.
By Mathlib's locally dominated parameter differentiation theorem (`mellin_hasDerivAt_of_isBigO_rpow`), the derivative of the Mellin transform is the Mellin transform of $\log x \cdot H_1(x)$:
$$
\mathcal{M}(\log x \cdot H_1(x))(s) = \frac{d}{ds}\left( -\frac{1}{s} \right) = \frac{1}{s^2}.
$$

### 2.2. Shifted Power Transforms
Applying the Mellin translation property $\mathcal{M}(x^a f(x))(s) = \mathcal{M}(f)(s+a)$:
$$
\mathcal{M}(x^2 H_1(x))(s) = -\frac{1}{s+2}, \quad \mathcal{M}(x^2 \log x H_1(x))(s) = \frac{1}{(s+2)^2}.
$$

### 2.3. Full Baseline Synthesis
Decomposing $B(x)$ linearly:
$$
B(x) = \frac{1}{2} x^2 \log x H_1(x) + \frac{1}{4} x^2 H_1(x) - \frac{1}{4} H_1(x).
$$
Evaluating at the argument $-s - 1$ where $\text{Re}(s) > 1$ (so $\text{Re}(-s-1+2) = 1 - \text{Re}(s) < 0$ and $\text{Re}(-s-1) < 0$):
$$
\mathcal{M}(B)(-s-1) = \frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{1}{(-s-1+2)^2} \right) + \frac{1}{4}\left( -\frac{1}{-s-1+2} \right) - \frac{1}{4}\left( -\frac{1}{-s-1} \right)
$$
$$
= \frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{(1-s)^2} - \frac{1}{4}\frac{1}{1-s} + \frac{1}{4}\frac{1}{-s-1}
$$
$$
= \frac{1}{2(s-1)^2} + \frac{1}{4(s-1)} - \frac{1}{4(s+1)}.
$$
Combining these partial fractions over a common denominator:
$$
\frac{2(s+1) + (s-1)(s+1) - (s-1)^2}{4(s-1)^2(s+1)} = \frac{2s + 2 + (s^2 - 1) - (s^2 - 2s + 1)}{4(s-1)^2(s+1)} = \frac{4s}{4(s-1)^2(s+1)} = \frac{s}{(s-1)^2(s+1)}.
$$
