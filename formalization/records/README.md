# Demoted research records

These records preserve mathematical statements and attribution notes that were
formerly catalogued as unique contributions or first formalizations. They are
kept as research documentation after the September 2026 taxonomy audit. A
record here is not a novelty or firstness claim: the public catalogs retain
only entries with an exact statement, a checked proof or certificate, a clear
provenance, and a bounded source comparison supporting its classification.

The corresponding Lean, certificate, and written mathematics artifacts remain
in their original subject directories. The retained public catalogs are
[unique contributions](../../unique-contributions/) and
[first formalizations](../../first-formalizations/).
